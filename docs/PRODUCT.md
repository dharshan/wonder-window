# Product

Wonder Window makes screen time more meaningful: discover, think, practise and experience.

## Home order

Decided in issue #1 (docs/DECISIONS.md D1); the app's home screen shows the doors in this order.

1. PLAY — first-party interactive educational games.
2. TRY — first-party offline real-world activities.
3. WATCH — provider-neutral third-party video integrations.

## PLAY
Short self-contained games for ages 3–10 using standard TV remote interaction. Support thinking, creativity, literacy, numeracy, discovery and problem-solving without competitive pressure.

## TRY
Movement, making, nature, thinking, everyday help, family connection and calm activities. TV gives a concise invitation/instruction, then the child steps away. The TV need not remain on.

## WATCH

Session length is a fixed parent setting inside the one shared daily budget; no quiz or warm-up changes it (D6).

Respect provider terms, rights, privacy, age suitability and playback rules. No scraping, bypassing, downloading or re-hosting without explicit rights.

Pillar connections are optional, never mandatory. Age bands 3–4, 5–6, 7–8, 9–10 are editorial adaptation bands, not universal developmental claims.

Non-goals: ads, purchases, child accounts, public chat, leaderboards, streaks, loot boxes, engagement manipulation, mandatory cloud, social network or arbitrary community-code marketplace.
