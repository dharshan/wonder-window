# Child safety and privacy

Core PLAY/TRY are offline-first and need no account. Do not collect child personal details or add analytics, ad identifiers, cross-app tracking, public chat or unnecessary network access.

Every child-facing item needs age band, clear goal/instructions, accessibility, provenance/licence, review state and reviewer notes. TRY also needs materials, foreseeable hazards, supervision, alternatives and stop conditions.

Review states: draft (not shown), needs_review (not shown), approved, parent_supervision_required (only with explicit adult supervision), rejected. Schema validation is not safety approval; human review is mandatory.

Avoid storing child names, DOBs, photos, voice, location, school or behavioural profiles. Keep local state minimal and parent-controlled.
