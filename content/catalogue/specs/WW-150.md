# WW-150 · Invent Adventure (our id `ww150`)
Status: **design_spec_draft** (awaiting father/reviewer approval) · Category: E. Creativity & Imagination / Imaginative Exploration · Variant: `pick`

**1. Identity.** Original title "Invent Adventure", catalogue id WW-150. Source record unchanged in master.json.

**2. Age band.** Record 4-6; fits him exactly.

**3. Objective (observable).** He is given a small imaginary situation (a magic door, a dragon, a magic seed) and chooses the idea he likes best from 3 pictures; every choice is accepted.

**4. What he practises.** Imagination and making a choice for his own reasons; listening to a short story setup; finishing a story with his own idea. There are no wrong answers, so there is nothing to fear. (Source objective: "Explore imaginative scenarios and make creative choices".)

**5. Originality and dedup.** Dedup cluster WW-DUP-018 (Dream Puzzle WW-207, Art Story WW-227 are in later batches). Best Tool (ww028) and What Comes Next? (ww008) have one correct answer; All About Me (core) asks about him. Here the scenario is imaginary and every pick is valid, so it is a different objective. The source says 'create' (a free-creation variant); a pick round where every choice is valid (answer + `also_ok` for all the rest, no `fade`) delivers the objective with existing variants. Decision: **own game**. Engine reviewer to confirm that an all-valid pick round is accepted and that a first press ends the round without a wobble.

**6. Core loop.** A scenario is spoken (🔊) and shown as a picture on the board with ❓. Three pictures offer ideas; whichever he picks, the `why` line celebrates it and the scene fills. Round-by-round the question changes: L1 where, L2 what to add, L3 what happens next.
Example rounds:
- L1 `ww150-1-01`: "Where could it go?", board 🚪 ❓, choices 🌊 ⭐ 🌳, every choice is fine. Spoken: "the buddy (Pip) finds a magic door. Where could it go? To the sea, to the stars, or to a garden?"
- L2 `ww150-2-01`: "What should we add?", board 🚣 ❓, choices 🚩 💡 🎵, every choice is fine. Spoken: "We built a little boat. What should we add? A flag, lights, or music?"
- L3 `ww150-3-01`: "What happens next?", board 🎈 ❓, choices 🐦 ☁️ 🦋, every choice is fine. Spoken: "the buddy (Pip)'s balloon floats up, up, up. Who does it meet? A bird, a cloud, or a butterfly?"

**7. Child-facing instruction.** Visual demo by the buddy (Pip) in round 1 of the first session. Narration only on 🔊: "Let's dream up an adventure! There is no wrong idea. Choose the one you like!" Prompt text 8 words or fewer (he can't read; it is for the parent).

**8. Remote mapping.** LEFT/RIGHT over the choices, OK confirms, UP 🔊, BACK pause. Initial focus: 🔊 (pulsing) on the first round, then the first choice. Focus never lands on an inert element; exactly one element is focused. During a peek the input is ignored except BACK and UP.

**9. Difficulty.** L1 'Where could it go?' (places and things, 3 ideas). L2 'What should we add?' (add something to what we made). L3 'What happens next?' (the next step of a small story). No score and no right answer; difficulty is only in how much he has to imagine. Levels: L1 Pick an idea 💭; L2 Make it better 🛠️; L3 What happens next? 🔮.

**10. Hint / retry / recovery.** There are no wrong picks, so a miss never happens. If he waits, the voice (🔊 only) repeats the idea options. Nothing is lost.

**11. Success, session end, early exit.** Session = 5 rounds, then the end_lines and the Play again / Back to games buttons. Early exit any time via BACK: it cancels any local action first, then opens the pause sheet; a second BACK returns to the host. No confirmation is needed because no progress is lost.

**12. Feedback.** Matches the record ("Gentle, positive visual feedback on success. Neutral and constructive response to errors with opportunity to try again. No pressure, timers, or negative messaging."). Correct: one of 6 `cheers`, the scene fills (🚪), soft `correct` sound. Miss: neutral wobble plus `tryagain` taps; never red, never "wrong".

**13. Session time.** About 20-35 s per round; 5 rounds take about 2-3 min. Target 1.5-4 min (record estimate 1.5-4 min).

**14. Assets / audio / content.** Large emoji idea cards, a sparkle on the scene when he picks. All spoken lines are pre-recorded clips (Rishi), with voice only after 🔊. Content: `catalogue/catalogue_games.json` → `ww150` (30 rounds, 3 levels).

**15. Accessibility.** Meaning is carried by the pictures, not by colour alone; labels are screen text for the parent only. No timers, no comparison with others, no 'best' choice. Plus: focus ring #F59E0B 8 px plus glow, targets at least 240 px, reduced-motion flag honoured, all audio optional, no reading required.

**16. Offline / failure.** Fully bundled; no network. If content fails to load or validate, the card is hidden and an error screen returns to the host.

**17. Parent options / data.** Enable/disable, starting level, reset. Stored locally: current level per game only. Reset clears it without touching time settings.

**18. Tests.** Validator: `validate_games.py` (schema, answer keys, 8-word prompts, Unicode 11 emoji, TTS rules). Each round has one defensible answer (plus `also_ok` where listed); choices distinct; labels match choices; `fade` is never a right answer. Every round: `answer` 0 and `also_ok` covers all other choices; no `fade`; every choice has a label; no scary pictures (no monsters that frighten). Plus host contract tests: launch blocked when disabled or out of budget; budget expiry in instructions, play, feedback and pause leads to a calm end; BACK in every state; cold start in airplane mode; a real-remote pass on the OnePlus TV.

**19. Evidence and limits.** Background: imagination and creative play are an early-years goal (NCF-FS, NCERT 2022, creative expression). Limitation: framework, not effect evidence; we do not claim a creativity gain. We claim only practice of the skill inside the activity.
