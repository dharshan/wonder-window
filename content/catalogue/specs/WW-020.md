# WW-020 · Safe Crossing (our id `ww020`)
Status: **design_spec_draft** (awaiting father/reviewer approval) · Category: J. Real-World Knowledge & Life Skills / Road & Pedestrian Safety · Variant: `pick`

**1. Identity.** Original title "Safe Crossing", catalogue id WW-020. Source record unchanged in master.json.

**2. Age band.** Record 5-7 / 5-8; fits him. The content stresses that at 5-6 he always crosses with a grown-up.

**3. Objective (observable).** In a simple road picture, he chooses the safest action from 3 cards (stop and wait, look both ways, hold a hand, use the crossing, footpath).

**4. What he practises.** Naming safe pedestrian behaviours in Bangalore street situations (autos, buses, cows, parked cars, ambulances). (Source objective: "Identify safe behaviours when crossing a road or moving near traffic".)

**5. Originality and dedup.** All About Me covers being lost and strangers; nothing covers road safety. Separate. Decision: **own game**.

**6. Core loop.** Street scene board; 3 action cards with labels. Safe card: Cherry walks safely across with Amma. Other: calm 'Let's think: is that slow and careful?', and another try.
Example rounds:
- L1 `ww020-1-01`: "What is the safe thing?", board 🚗 🛣️; choices ⚽ 🛑 🏃; answer 🛑. Spoken: "A car is coming down the road. What is the safe thing to do? Chase the ball, stop and wait, or run across?"
- L2 `ww020-2-01`: "What is the safe thing?", board 🚙 🛣️; choices 👀 🏃 🙈; answer 👀. Spoken: "A parked car hides the road. You can't see. What is the safe thing to do? Look both ways, run across, or close your eyes?"
- L3 `ww020-3-01`: "What is the safe thing?", board 🚑 🔊; choices 🏃 🛑 🙈; answer 🛑. Spoken: "An ambulance with a siren is coming. What is the safe thing to do? Run across, stop and wait, or close your eyes?"

**7. Child-facing instruction.** Visual demo by Cherry in round 1 of the first session. Narration only on 🔊: "Roads are busy. Let's learn the safe things to do, always with a grown-up." Prompt text 8 words or fewer (he can't read; it is for the parent).

**8. Remote mapping.** Row of 3 cards, LEFT/RIGHT, OK, UP 🔊, BACK pause. Initial focus: 🔊 (pulsing) on the first round, then the first choice. Focus never lands on an inert element; exactly one element is focused.

**9. Difficulty.** L1 clear safe vs unsafe, L2 hidden views and slippery roads, L3 sirens, changing lights, blocked footpaths. Levels: L1 Clear safe choices 🛑, L2 Think it through 👀, L3 Tricky roads 🚸.

**10. Hint / retry / recovery.** Miss: 'Safe means slow, careful, and with a grown-up'; no crash imagery, no fear language. 🔊 always replays the line. After the second miss the answer is shown and he presses it ("You kept going!"). Nothing is lost.

**11. Success, session end, early exit.** 5 situations. Each set is 5 rounds. BACK cancels any local action first (palette, peek), then opens the pause sheet; a second BACK returns to the host. No confirmation is needed because no progress is lost.

**12. Feedback.** Matches the record ("Positive confirmation of safe choice. Calm, clear explanation of why another choice is less safe (no fear-based messaging)."). Correct: one of 6 `cheers`, the scene fills (🚦), soft `correct` sound. Miss: neutral wobble plus `tryagain` taps; never red, never "wrong".

**13. Session time.** About 20-30 s per round; about 2-2.5 min. Target 1.5-4 min (record estimate 1.5-3 min).

**14. Assets / audio / content.** Road scene cards (🛣️ 🚦 🦓 🚕 🚌), action cards. All spoken lines are pre-recorded clips (Rishi), with voice only after 🔊. Content: `catalogue/catalogue_games.json` → `ww020`.

**15. Accessibility.** Labels on cards; calm tone; no flashing. Plus: focus ring #F59E0B 8 px plus glow, targets at least 240 px, reduced-motion flag honoured, all audio optional, no reading required.

**16. Offline / failure.** Fully bundled; no network. If content fails to load or validate, the card is hidden and an error screen returns to the host.

**17. Parent options / data.** Enable/disable, reset. Suggest practising on real walks. Stored locally: current level per game only. Reset clears it without touching time settings.

**18. Tests.** Validator: `validate_games.py` (schema, answer keys, 8-word prompts, Unicode 11 emoji, TTS rules). No content implies a child should cross alone; every 'why' line is non-fearful; reviewed by the parent. Plus host contract tests: launch blocked when disabled or out of budget; budget expiry in instructions, play, feedback and pause leads to a calm end; BACK in every state; cold start in airplane mode; a real-remote pass on the OnePlus TV.

**19. Evidence and limits.** WHO (2015), Ten Strategies for Keeping Children Safe on the Road: young children cannot reliably judge traffic and need adult supervision. Limitation: knowing the rule on screen does not ensure safe behaviour; practise with an adult. We claim only practice of the skill inside the activity.
