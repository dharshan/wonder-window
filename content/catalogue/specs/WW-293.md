# WW-293 · Five Senses (our id `ww293`)
Status: **design_spec_draft** (awaiting father/reviewer approval) · Category: F. Science & Discovery / Five Senses · Variant: `pick`

**1. Identity.** Original title "Nature Puzzle" (a catalogue artefact), renamed "Five Senses"; catalogue id WW-293. Source record unchanged in master.json.

**2. Age band.** Record 5-7; fits him at L1.

**3. Objective (observable).** He names which sense does a job: first which body part (eyes, ears, nose, tongue, hands), then which one fits a picture (drum, flower, lemon), then trickier cases where two or three senses help.

**4. What he practises.** Linking the five senses to their body parts and to everyday experiences; noticing that some things use several senses. (Source objective: "Identify which sense is being used in different situations".)

**5. Originality and dedup.** Wonder Lab has one round ('What helps you smell?') and no game covers the senses as a topic; Animal Needs and Plant Helper are other science topics. Cluster WW-DUP-089 (WW-495 Science Game) merges here. Same action as other pick games, new content and objective, so a new game. Decision: **own game** (reviewer to confirm).

**6. Core loop.** A question names a job or shows a thing; he picks eyes 👀, ears 👂, nose 👃, tongue 👅 or hands ✋. L3 has rounds with several right answers (`also_ok`).
Example rounds:
- L1 `ww293-1-01`: "What do we use?", board , choices 👀 👃 👂. Spoken: "What do we use to see? Our eyes, nose, or ears?"
- L2 `ww293-2-01`: "Which sense do we use?", board 🥁, choices 👃 👀 👂. Spoken: "Boom boom! You hear a big drum. What do you use?"
- L3 `ww293-3-01`: "Which sense helps?", board 🥭, choices 👂 👅 👃 👀. Spoken: "A ripe mango on the plate. What can we use to enjoy it? Our ears, tongue, nose, or eyes?"

**7. Child-facing instruction.** Visual demo by the buddy (Pip) in round 1 of the first session. Narration only on 🔊: "the buddy (Pip) can see, hear, smell, taste, and touch! Which part of us does each job?" Prompt text 8 words or fewer (he can't read; it is for the parent).

**8. Remote mapping.** LEFT/RIGHT over the choices, OK confirms, UP 🔊, BACK pause. Initial focus: 🔊 (pulsing) on the first round, then the first choice. Focus never lands on an inert element; exactly one element is focused.

**9. Difficulty.** L1 ten body-part questions, three choices. L2 ten pictures, three choices. L3 ten thinking rounds with 3-4 choices, some with two or three right answers. Levels: L1 Our five senses 👀; L2 What do we use? 👃; L3 Think it through 🧠.

**10. Hint / retry / recovery.** After 1 miss the choice fades and the `hint` is shown (voice only on 🔊). After 2 misses the answer is shown and he presses it ("You kept going!"). 🔊 always replays the line. Nothing is lost. Taste rounds use only safe foods (mango, cake, chocolate, ice cream); nothing asks him to taste unknown things.

**11. Success, session end, early exit.** Session = 5 rounds, then the end_lines and the Play again / Back to games buttons. BACK cancels any local action first, then opens the pause sheet; a second BACK returns to the host. No confirmation is needed because no progress is lost.

**12. Feedback.** Correct: one of 6 `cheers`, the scene fills (👃), soft `correct` sound. Miss: neutral wobble plus `tryagain` taps; never red, never "wrong"; the `why` line is a calm fact, never a scolding.

**13. Session time.** About 25-40 s per round; about 2-3 min per set. Target 1.5-4 min.

**14. Assets / audio / content.** Emoji pictures with labels (screen text). All spoken lines are pre-recorded clips (Rishi), voice only after 🔊. Content: `catalogue/catalogue_games.json` -> `ww293` (30 rounds, 3 levels; generator tools/b10/).

**15. Accessibility.** Pictures carry meaning; labels optional; focus ring #F59E0B 8 px plus glow, targets at least 240 px, reduced-motion flag honoured, all audio optional, no reading required.

**16. Offline / failure.** Fully bundled; no network. If content fails to load or validate, the card is hidden and an error screen returns to the host.

**17. Parent options / data.** Enable/disable, rounds per set (4 or 5), reset. Stored locally: current level per game only.

**18. Tests.** Validator `validate_games.py` (schema, answer keys, 8-word prompts, Unicode 11 emoji, TTS rules, `also_ok`, `fade`). Each round has one defensible answer (or listed `also_ok`); choices distinct; labels match choices. Plus the host contract tests (launch blocked when out of budget, BACK in every state, cold start offline, real-remote pass).

**19. Evidence and limits.** The five senses are a standard early-years science topic (NCF-FS, NCERT 2022). Framework, not effect evidence; we claim only practice of the skill inside the activity.
