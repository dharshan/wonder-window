# WW-213 · Design Adventure
Status: **deferred** · Category: E. Creativity & Imagination / Story Creation · Source age 5-6 · Variant: `compose (new)`

**Objective (source).** Create a simple original story using picture prompts

**Decision.** **deferred: needs variant `compose`**.

**Objective (observable).** He makes his own little story by choosing 3 pictures (who, where, what happens) and Cherry tells it back.

**What he practises.** Telling an original story from picture prompts; choosing and combining ideas; retelling. (Source objective: "Create a simple original story using picture prompts".)

**Originality and dedup.** Invent Adventure (ww150) is one all-valid choice per round with no story built; Story Steps (ww017) orders a given story. Here the story is made of his three picks and replayed as a whole, which needs the slot cycler of `compose` mode `slots` (WW-141). Cluster WW-DUP-056 (WW-258 Design Puzzle) and WW-210 (7-8, same objective) follow this spec. Decision: **deferred: needs variant `compose`**.

**Schema proposal.** Variant `compose`, mode `slots` (specs/WW-133.md, WW-141): slots `who` (3-5 characters), `where` (3-5 places), `what` (3-5 actions), `any_ok` true, and a new `story` template string with the slot names, spoken on 🔊 only, e.g. "{who} goes to the {where} and {what}!". Placeholders are filled by the engine from the chosen labels; each option needs a label. Never wrong, nothing saved.

**Age, remote, hints.** Source age 5-6. LEFT/RIGHT/UP/DOWN to move, OK to choose or place, BACK undo then pause; no timers, no wrong answers; fully bundled and offline. No game content was written in batch b9.
