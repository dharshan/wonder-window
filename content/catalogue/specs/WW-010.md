# WW-010 · Kind Choice (our id `ww010`)
Status: **design_spec_draft** (awaiting father/reviewer approval) · Category: I. Emotional Intelligence & Social Learning / Prosocial Decision Making · Variant: `pick`

**1. Identity.** Original title "Kind Choice", catalogue id WW-010. Source record unchanged in master.json.

**2. Age band.** Record 5-7 / 5-8; fits him.

**3. Objective (observable).** In a short picture situation, he chooses a kind or helpful action from 3-4 action cards (several can be acceptable).

**4. What he practises.** Noticing others' needs and naming helpful actions (help, share, comfort, take turns, tell a grown-up). (Source objective: "Select a kind or helpful response in a simple social situation".)

**5. Originality and dedup.** Feeling Faces names feelings; All About Me has safety. None offers prosocial action choices. Separate. Decision: **own game**.

**6. Core loop.** Situation board, action cards with labels. Kind choices (answer or also_ok) fill the kindness jar. Other choices get a calm 'Hmm, how would they feel?' and another try.
Example rounds:
- L1 `ww010-1-01`: "What is the kind thing?", board 👧 🖍️ ⬇️; choices 🤝 🚶 😂; answer 🤝. Spoken: "Your friend drops her crayons. What could you do? Help, walk away, or laugh?"
- L2 `ww010-2-01`: "What is the kind thing?", board 🧒 🧩; choices 🤝 🏃 😂 🤗; answer 🤝. Spoken: "Your friend can't finish a puzzle and feels cross. What could you do? Help, run off, laugh, or give a hug?"
- L3 `ww010-3-01`: "What is the kind thing?", board 🧒 🏆 🧒; choices 🗣️ 🤝 🤗 😂; answer 🤗. Spoken: "You win a race. Your friend comes last and looks sad. What could you do? Shout, help, give a hug, or laugh?"

**7. Child-facing instruction.** Visual demo by Cherry in round 1 of the first session. Narration only on 🔊: "Little things can be kind. Let's think about what we could do to help." Prompt text 8 words or fewer (he can't read; it is for the parent).

**8. Remote mapping.** Row of 3-4 cards, LEFT/RIGHT, OK, UP 🔊, BACK pause. Initial focus: 🔊 (pulsing) on the first round, then the first choice. Focus never lands on an inert element; exactly one element is focused.

**9. Difficulty.** L1 clear choices, L2 thinking of others, L3 tricky moments (winning, waiting, accepting sorry). Levels: L1 Easy kind choices 🌱, L2 Thinking of others 💭, L3 Tricky moments 🤔.

**10. Hint / retry / recovery.** After a less kind choice: 'How would they feel?', and the person's face in the board turns sad then hopeful. Never shaming. 🔊 always replays the line. After the second miss the answer is shown and he presses it ("You kept going!"). Nothing is lost.

**11. Success, session end, early exit.** 5 situations. Each set is 5 rounds. BACK cancels any local action first (palette, peek), then opens the pause sheet; a second BACK returns to the host. No confirmation is needed because no progress is lost.

**12. Feedback.** Matches the record ("Gentle positive acknowledgment of kind choices. Neutral, non-shaming feedback on other choices with option to reconsider."). Correct: one of 6 `cheers`, the scene fills (💗), soft `correct` sound. Miss: neutral wobble plus `tryagain` taps; never red, never "wrong".

**13. Session time.** About 20-30 s per round; about 2-2.5 min. Target 1.5-4 min (record estimate 1.5-3 min).

**14. Assets / audio / content.** Emoji people and situations; a kindness jar that fills with hearts. All spoken lines are pre-recorded clips (Rishi), with voice only after 🔊. Content: `catalogue/catalogue_games.json` → `ww010`.

**15. Accessibility.** Labels on every card; voice optional; neutral colours (no red for 'unkind'). Plus: focus ring #F59E0B 8 px plus glow, targets at least 240 px, reduced-motion flag honoured, all audio optional, no reading required.

**16. Offline / failure.** Fully bundled; no network. If content fails to load or validate, the card is hidden and an error screen returns to the host.

**17. Parent options / data.** Enable/disable, reset. Stored locally: current level per game only. Reset clears it without touching time settings.

**18. Tests.** Validator: `validate_games.py` (schema, answer keys, 8-word prompts, Unicode 11 emoji, TTS rules). also_ok indexes valid; no option is mocking in tone; reviewed by the parent for values. Plus host contract tests: launch blocked when disabled or out of budget; budget expiry in instructions, play, feedback and pause leads to a calm end; BACK in every state; cold start in airplane mode; a real-remote pass on the OnePlus TV.

**19. Evidence and limits.** Eisenberg, N., Spinrad, T. L. & Knafo-Noam, A. (2015), 'Prosocial development', Handbook of Child Psychology and Developmental Science. Limitation: choosing a kind card on screen is not the same as acting kindly; Try activities can bridge this. We claim only practice of the skill inside the activity.
