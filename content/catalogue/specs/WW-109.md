# WW-109 · Animal Needs (our id `ww109`)
Status: **design_spec_draft** (awaiting father/reviewer approval) · Category: F. Science & Discovery / Animals & Habitats · Variant: `pick`

**1. Identity.** Original title "Animal Needs", catalogue id WW-109. Source record unchanged in master.json.

**2. Age band.** Record 4-6; fits him exactly.

**3. Objective (observable).** He picks what an animal needs: food, water, a safe place to rest, a gentle touch (L1-L2), then thinks about caring for pets and wild animals, including 'ask a grown-up' and 'go to the vet' (L3).

**4. What he practises.** Knowing what living things need to stay well; caring and kindness towards animals; safe habits around animals. (Source objective: "Choose what an animal needs (food, water, shelter)".)

**5. Originality and dedup.** Plant Helper (ww034) is about what plants need; Animal Homes (mode sort-animal-homes) sorts animals into where they live; Care for Nature (ww091) is about actions towards plants and the earth. This is what animals need and how to care for a pet, a different subject and objective. Same pick action, but no existing round asks what an animal needs. Decision: **own game** (reviewer to confirm it is not too close to Plant Helper).

**6. Core loop.** An animal sits on the board with its need in the spoken line (hungry, thirsty, tired). He picks the thing it needs. L3 asks about two needs, several valid answers, and safe choices (ask a grown-up, go to the vet).
Example rounds:
- L1 `ww109-1-01`: "What does it need?", board 🐶, choices 🎩 🍖 📖, answer 🍖. Spoken: "The puppy is hungry. What does it need? Food, a book, or a hat?"
- L2 `ww109-2-01`: "What does it need?", board 🐱, choices 🥤 🍵 💧, answer 💧. Spoken: "The cat is thirsty. What is best for it? Fresh water, a cold drink, or hot tea?"
- L3 `ww109-3-01`: "What does it need?", board 🐟, choices 💧🍽️ 🚗🥁 🍬🍟, answer 💧🍽️. Spoken: "A pet fish needs two things. Which two? Clean water and fish food, sweets and chips, or a toy car and a drum?"

**7. Child-facing instruction.** Visual demo by Cherry in round 1 of the first session. Narration only on 🔊: "Cherry has animal friends. Each one needs something. Can you choose what it needs?" Prompt text 8 words or fewer (he can't read; it is for the parent).

**8. Remote mapping.** LEFT/RIGHT over the choices, OK confirms, UP 🔊, BACK pause. Initial focus: 🔊 (pulsing) on the first round, then the first choice. Focus never lands on an inert element; exactly one element is focused.

**9. Difficulty.** L1 three choices, obvious need (hungry, thirsty, sleepy) with silly distractors. L2 three choices, the right food or place for each animal. L3 three to four choices, some with more than one right answer (`also_ok`) and safe-behaviour rounds. Levels: L1 Food, water, home 🐶; L2 What suits each one 🐄; L3 Caring for animals 🤗.

**10. Hint / retry / recovery.** After 1 miss the choice fades and the `hint` is shown (voice only on 🔊). After 2 misses the answer is shown and he presses it ("You kept going!"). 🔊 always replays the line. Nothing is lost.

**11. Success, session end, early exit.** Session = 5 rounds, then the end_lines and the Play again / Back to games buttons. Early exit any time via BACK: it cancels any local action first, then opens the pause sheet; a second BACK returns to the host. No confirmation is needed because no progress is lost.

**12. Feedback.** Matches the record ("Soft positive confirmation. Errors receive neutral feedback and the chance to try again. No negative sounds or messages."). Correct: one of 6 `cheers`, the scene fills (🐾), soft `correct` sound. Miss: neutral wobble plus `tryagain` taps; never red, never "wrong".

**13. Session time.** About 20-35 s per round; 5 rounds take about 2-3 min. Target 1.5-4 min (record estimate 1.5-4 min).

**14. Assets / audio / content.** Animal emoji on the board; large candy-card choices; no sad or hurt animals. All spoken lines are pre-recorded clips (Rishi), with voice only after 🔊. Content: `catalogue/catalogue_games.json` → `ww109` (30 rounds, 3 levels).

**15. Accessibility.** Meaning is carried by the pictures, not by colour alone; labels are screen text for the parent only. Every round that touches a stray or wild animal says to ask a grown-up; animals are never hurt on screen; no pet-keeping advice that could cause harm (no 'keep a bird in a cage'). Plus: focus ring #F59E0B 8 px plus glow, targets at least 240 px, reduced-motion flag honoured, all audio optional, no reading required.

**16. Offline / failure.** Fully bundled; no network. If content fails to load or validate, the card is hidden and an error screen returns to the host.

**17. Parent options / data.** Enable/disable, starting level, reset. Stored locally: current level per game only. Reset clears it without touching time settings.

**18. Tests.** Validator: `validate_games.py` (schema, answer keys, 8-word prompts, Unicode 11 emoji, TTS rules). Each round has one defensible answer (plus `also_ok` where listed); choices distinct; labels match choices; `fade` is never a right answer. Plus host contract tests: launch blocked when disabled or out of budget; budget expiry in instructions, play, feedback and pause leads to a calm end; BACK in every state; cold start in airplane mode; a real-remote pass on the OnePlus TV.

**19. Evidence and limits.** Background: caring for living things is an early-years science and values goal (NCF-FS, NCERT 2022). Limitation: framework, not effect evidence. We claim only practice of the skill inside the activity.
