# WW-006 · Feeling Faces (our id `ww006`)
Status: **design_spec_draft** (awaiting father/reviewer approval) · Category: I. Emotional Intelligence & Social Learning / Emotion Recognition · Variant: `pick`

**1. Identity.** Original title "Feeling Faces", catalogue id WW-006. Source record unchanged in master.json.

**2. Age band.** Record 3-5 / 3-6; fits him (UKG emotions vocabulary). L3 situations suit 5-6.

**3. Objective (observable).** He names the feeling shown by a face (L1-L2) or the likely feeling in a short picture situation (L3), choosing from 3-4 labelled faces.

**4. What he practises.** Recognising happy, sad, angry, surprised and scared across different face drawings; linking situations to feelings. (Source objective: "Recognise basic facial expressions and match them to emotion labels".)

**5. Originality and dedup.** All About Me has 3 feeling rounds as part of self-knowledge; this game's objective is emotion recognition itself, so it stays separate. Kind Choice (WW-010) is about actions, not feelings. Decision: **own game**.

**6. Core loop.** A big face (cat or varied style) on a mirror; choose the human face with the same feeling. In L3, a situation picture is shown first.
Example rounds:
- L1 `ww006-1-01`: "How does this face feel?", board 😸; choices 😢 😠 😀; answer 😀. Spoken: "Look at this face. Is it Sad, angry, or happy?"
- L2 `ww006-2-01`: "How does this face feel?", board 🙀; choices 😠 😮 😢 😀; answer 😮. Spoken: "Look at this face. Is it Angry, surprised, sad, or happy?"
- L3 `ww006-3-01`: "How would you feel?", board 🎁; choices 😮 😠 😀 😢; answer 😀. Spoken: "You get a present from Thatha! How might you feel? Surprised, angry, happy, or sad?"

**7. Child-facing instruction.** Visual demo by Cherry in round 1 of the first session. Narration only on 🔊: "Faces tell us how people feel. Let's look at faces together!" Prompt text 8 words or fewer (he can't read; it is for the parent).

**8. Remote mapping.** Row of 3-4 labelled face cards, LEFT/RIGHT, OK; UP goes to 🔊; BACK goes to the pause sheet. Initial focus: 🔊 (pulsing) on the first round, then the first choice. Focus never lands on an inert element; exactly one element is focused.

**9. Difficulty.** L1 happy/sad/angry, L2 + surprised/scared, L3 situations (some accept two feelings, via also_ok). Same in-set rule. Levels: L1 Happy, sad, angry 😀, L2 Surprised and scared 😮, L3 Feelings stories 📖.

**10. Hint / retry / recovery.** Miss: 'Look at the eyes and the mouth', and the face's mouth and eyebrows get a soft outline. No 'wrong'. 🔊 always replays the line. After the second miss the answer is shown and he presses it ("You kept going!"). Nothing is lost.

**11. Success, session end, early exit.** 5 rounds; the end line asks how he feels now (no input stored). Each set is 5 rounds. BACK cancels any local action first (palette, peek), then opens the pause sheet; a second BACK returns to the host. No confirmation is needed because no progress is lost.

**12. Feedback.** Matches the record ("Soft positive confirmation. On incorrect, gentle neutral feedback and option to look again."). Correct: one of 6 `cheers`, the scene fills (🎭), soft `correct` sound. Miss: neutral wobble plus `tryagain` taps; never red, never "wrong".

**13. Session time.** About 15-25 s per round; about 2 min per set. Target 1.5-4 min (record estimate 1-2.5 min).

**14. Assets / audio / content.** Emoji faces (Unicode 11 or lower), a mirror frame, feeling labels under the faces. All spoken lines are pre-recorded clips (Rishi), with voice only after 🔊. Content: `catalogue/catalogue_games.json` → `ww006`.

**15. Accessibility.** Labels plus faces (not colour). Calm sounds. Reduced motion supported. Plus: focus ring #F59E0B 8 px plus glow, targets at least 240 px, reduced-motion flag honoured, all audio optional, no reading required.

**16. Offline / failure.** Fully bundled; no network. If content fails to load or validate, the card is hidden and an error screen returns to the host.

**17. Parent options / data.** Enable/disable, emotion set (hide 'scared' if the parent prefers), reset. Stored locally: current level per game only. Reset clears it without touching time settings.

**18. Tests.** Validator: `validate_games.py` (schema, answer keys, 8-word prompts, Unicode 11 emoji, TTS rules). also_ok answers count as correct; situations reviewed for cultural fit; no frightening imagery. Plus host contract tests: launch blocked when disabled or out of budget; budget expiry in instructions, play, feedback and pause leads to a calm end; BACK in every state; cold start in airplane mode; a real-remote pass on the OnePlus TV.

**19. Evidence and limits.** Denham, S. A. (1998), Emotional Development in Young Children (Guilford): emotion knowledge in preschoolers. Limitation: emoji are stylised; recognising them is not the same as reading real faces in context. We claim only practice of the skill inside the activity.
