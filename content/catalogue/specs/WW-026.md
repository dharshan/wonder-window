**1. Identity.** Original title "Sink or Float", catalogue id WW-026. Source record unchanged in master.json. Decision: **own game**.

**2. Age band.** Record says 5-7 primary / 5-7, 6-8. He is 5½: fits from L1.

**3. Objective (observable).** He predicts whether an object floats or sinks, then sees the answer and the reason. (Source objective: "Predict whether common objects will sink or float and observe the result".)

**4. What he practises.** Prediction, comparing weight and air, everyday science words.

**5. Originality and dedup.** Wonder Lab asks 'what happens' questions across topics; none is a dedicated predict-then-see game on floating. Different objective. Decision: **own game**.

**6. Core loop.** One round at a time, 3 levels of 10 rounds (30 total), 5 rounds per set (3 for a mode). Correct answer: a cheer, the scene fills, soft sound. Wrong: neutral wobble and a hint; no penalty.
Example rounds:
- L1 `ww026-1-01`: "Float or sink?" Spoken: "Will the balloon float, or sink? Think, then choose!"
- L3 `ww026-3-01`: "Float or sink?" Spoken: "Will the watermelon float, or sink? Think, then choose!"

**7. Child-facing instruction.** Visual demo by Cherry in round 1 of the first set. Narration only on 🔊: "Splash! Let's try things in Cherry's pond. Will it float, or sink?" Prompt text 8 words or fewer (he can't read; it is for the parent).

**8. Remote mapping.** LEFT/RIGHT (and UP/DOWN on the 2x2 grid) over the choices, OK answers, UP on the speaker for 🔊. BACK pauses (Keep playing / Back to games). Initial focus: 🔊 (pulsing) on the first round, then the first choice. Focus never lands on an inert element; exactly one element is focused.

**9. Difficulty.** L1 obvious (balloon, key), L2 kitchen things (apple, potato), L3 surprises (watermelon floats, paperclip sinks). In-set rule: 3 first-try in a row moves up one level, at most once per set; 2 misses in a row moves down silently. Levels: L1 Easy ones 🎈; L2 Kitchen things 🍎; L3 Big surprises 🍉.

**10. Hint / retry / recovery.** After 1 miss: the hint line plays (on 🔊) and one wrong choice fades. After 2 misses: the answer pulses and he presses it ("You kept going!"). 🔊 always replays the line. Nothing is lost.

**11. Success, session end, early exit.** 5 rounds per set, then the end_lines and Play again / Back to games. BACK cancels any local action first, then opens the pause sheet; a second BACK returns to the host. No confirmation is needed because no progress is lost.

**12. Feedback.** Correct: one of 6 `cheers`, the scene fills, soft `correct` sound. Miss: neutral wobble plus `tryagain` taps; never red, never "wrong". Record says: "Clear visual result. Calm statement of what happened. No 'wrong' messaging — focus on discovery."

**13. Session time.** About 20-40 s per round; a set of 5 rounds takes about 2-3 min. Target 1.5-4 min.

**14. Assets / audio / content.** Emoji pictures (Unicode 11 or older, no ZWJ) in white candy cards, Cherry the sparrow, soft sfx. All spoken lines are pre-recorded clips (Rishi), voice only after 🔊; sounds are written as buh, mmm, sss, never letter names. Content: `catalogue/batches/b2.json` → `ww026`.
