# WW-292 · Safe or Not Safe (our id `ww292`)
Status: **design_spec_draft** (awaiting father/reviewer approval) · Category: J. Real-World Knowledge & Life Skills / Personal Safety · Variant: `pick`

**1. Identity.** Original title "Healthy Trail" (a catalogue artefact), renamed "Safe or Not Safe"; catalogue id WW-292. Source record unchanged in master.json.

**2. Age band.** Record 4-6; fits him at L1.

**3. Objective (observable).** He decides whether an everyday situation is safe (L1, two choices), picks the safe thing to do (L2) and thinks through trickier cases with 3-4 choices, one with two safe answers (L3).

**4. What he practises.** Recognising common hazards at home and outside (heat, electricity, sharp things, water, medicine, heights) and the safe habit: ask or tell a grown-up, go slowly, wait. (Source objective: "Identify safe and unsafe situations in everyday contexts".)

**5. Originality and dedup.** Safe Crossing (ww020) covers roads only; Healthy Choice (ww089) is food and habits; Kind Choice (ww010) is manners; All About Me has one 'safe helper' round. No game covers hazards at home and outside, so this is new content, not a rename. Clusters WW-DUP-106 (WW-355, WW-415) and WW-346 merge here. Decision: **own game** (reviewer to confirm).

**6. Core loop.** A picture sets the scene; L1 asks 'safe, or not safe?' with a tick or a stop sign, L2 and L3 ask 'what is the safe thing?'. Calm tone: hazards are named, never shown scary; every why-line ends with what to do.
Example rounds:
- L1 `ww292-1-01`: "Is it safe?", board 🍳, choices ✅ 🚫. Spoken: "A pan is hot on the stove. Is it safe to touch it? Safe, or not safe?"
- L2 `ww292-2-01`: "What is the safe thing?", board 💊, choices 🥤 🎲 🙋. Spoken: "You find a bottle of medicine. What is safe to do? Ask a grown-up, drink it, or play with it?"
- L3 `ww292-3-01`: "What is the safe thing?", board 🌫️, choices 🚪 🛏️ 🔍. Spoken: "You smell smoke at home. What is safe to do? Go out and tell a grown-up, hide under the bed, or look for the fire?"

**7. Child-facing instruction.** Visual demo by the buddy (Pip) in round 1 of the first session. Narration only on 🔊: "the buddy (Pip) wants to stay safe. Help her choose what is safe!" Prompt text 8 words or fewer (he can't read; it is for the parent).

**8. Remote mapping.** LEFT/RIGHT over the choices, OK confirms, UP 🔊, BACK pause. Initial focus: 🔊 (pulsing) on the first round, then the first choice. Focus never lands on an inert element; exactly one element is focused.

**9. Difficulty.** L1 ten situations, two choices (safe or not safe, mixed about 4 to 6). L2 ten situations, 2-3 actions. L3 ten situations, 3-4 actions, one with two safe answers. Levels: L1 Safe or not safe? ✅; L2 What is safe to do? 🚸; L3 Think it through 🧠.

**10. Hint / retry / recovery.** After 1 miss the choice fades and the `hint` is shown (voice only on 🔊). After 2 misses the answer is shown and he presses it ("You kept going!"). 🔊 always replays the line. Nothing is lost. Rounds about getting lost are left to All About Me; no stranger scenes beyond 'say no thanks and tell Amma'.

**11. Success, session end, early exit.** Session = 5 rounds, then the end_lines and the Play again / Back to games buttons. BACK cancels any local action first, then opens the pause sheet; a second BACK returns to the host. No confirmation is needed because no progress is lost.

**12. Feedback.** Correct: one of 6 `cheers`, the scene fills (🚸), soft `correct` sound. Miss: neutral wobble plus `tryagain` taps; never red, never "wrong"; the `why` line is a calm fact, never a scolding.

**13. Session time.** About 25-40 s per round; about 2-3 min per set. Target 1.5-4 min.

**14. Assets / audio / content.** Emoji pictures with labels (screen text). All spoken lines are pre-recorded clips (Rishi), voice only after 🔊. Content: `catalogue/catalogue_games.json` -> `ww292` (30 rounds, 3 levels; generator tools/b10/).

**15. Accessibility.** Pictures carry meaning; labels optional; focus ring #F59E0B 8 px plus glow, targets at least 240 px, reduced-motion flag honoured, all audio optional, no reading required.

**16. Offline / failure.** Fully bundled; no network. If content fails to load or validate, the card is hidden and an error screen returns to the host.

**17. Parent options / data.** Enable/disable, rounds per set (4 or 5), reset. Stored locally: current level per game only.

**18. Tests.** Validator `validate_games.py` (schema, answer keys, 8-word prompts, Unicode 11 emoji, TTS rules, `also_ok`, `fade`). Each round has one defensible answer (or listed `also_ok`); choices distinct; labels match choices. Plus the host contract tests (launch blocked when out of budget, BACK in every state, cold start offline, real-remote pass).

**19. Evidence and limits.** Everyday safety is an early-years life-skills theme (NCF-FS, NCERT 2022). Framework, not effect evidence; we claim only practice of the skill inside the activity.
