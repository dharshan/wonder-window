# WW-089 · Healthy Choice (our id `ww089`)
Status: **design_spec_draft** (awaiting father/reviewer approval) · Category: J. Real-World Knowledge & Life Skills / Healthy Habits · Variant: `pick`

**1. Identity.** Original title "Healthy Choice", catalogue id WW-089. Source record unchanged in master.json.

**2. Age band.** Record 5-7; fits him (4-6 is fine for L1).

**3. Objective (observable).** He picks the healthier of two foods or habits (L1-L2), then the healthiest of three or four (L3), including a few rounds where two answers are both healthy.

**4. What he practises.** Linking everyday habits (food, water, sleep, washing, moving) to a strong body; weighing 'every day' against 'sometimes'. (Source objective: "Choose the healthier option between two activities or foods".)

**5. Originality and dedup.** Safe Crossing is road safety, Plant Helper is plants. The sort game 'Healthy Plate' (mode sort-healthy-plate) puts foods in healthy or treat homes; here he chooses between two real options, and includes habits (sleep, washing, moving) not only food. Same topic, different action and content. Decision: **own game** (reviewer to confirm).

**6. Core loop.** A context picture sets the scene (a sunny morning, bedtime). Two or more options appear; he picks the healthier one. Foods are never called 'bad': treats are 'for sometimes'.
Example rounds:
- L1 `ww089-1-01`: "Which is healthier?", board 🙂, choices 🍬 🍎, answer 🍎. Spoken: "Which is healthier for a strong body? An apple, or a candy?"
- L2 `ww089-2-01`: "Which is healthier?", board 🌞, choices ⚽ 📺, answer ⚽. Spoken: "It is a sunny morning. What is healthier? Playing outside, or watching television all day?"
- L3 `ww089-3-01`: "Which is healthiest?", board 🍽️, choices 🍟 🍌 🍪, answer 🍌. Spoken: "It is snack time. What is healthiest? A banana, chips, or biscuits?"

**7. Child-facing instruction.** Visual demo by Cherry in round 1 of the first session. Narration only on 🔊: "Cherry wants to stay strong and healthy. Help her pick the healthy choice!" Prompt text 8 words or fewer (he can't read; it is for the parent).

**8. Remote mapping.** LEFT/RIGHT over the choices, OK confirms, UP 🔊, BACK pause. Initial focus: 🔊 (pulsing) on the first round, then the first choice. Focus never lands on an inert element; exactly one element is focused.

**9. Difficulty.** L1 ten foods against treats (2 choices). L2 ten habits (2 choices). L3 three to four choices; some rounds have two healthy answers (`also_ok`). Levels: L1 Food choices 🍎; L2 Healthy habits 🦷; L3 Think it through 💪.

**10. Hint / retry / recovery.** After 1 miss the choice fades and the `hint` is shown (voice only on 🔊). After 2 misses the answer is shown and he presses it ("You kept going!"). 🔊 always replays the line. Nothing is lost.

**11. Success, session end, early exit.** Session = 5 rounds, then the end_lines and the Play again / Back to games buttons. Early exit any time via BACK: it cancels any local action first, then opens the pause sheet; a second BACK returns to the host. No confirmation is needed because no progress is lost.

**12. Feedback.** Matches the record ("Gentle positive visual confirmation on success. Neutral, non-punitive feedback on errors with option to try again. No timers or pressure."). Correct: one of 6 `cheers`, the scene fills (🥗), soft `correct` sound. Miss: neutral wobble plus `tryagain` taps; never red, never "wrong".

**13. Session time.** About 20-35 s per round; 5 rounds take about 2-3 min. Target 1.5-4 min (record estimate 1.5-3.5 min).

**14. Assets / audio / content.** Candy-card choices, scene emoji on the board, no pictures of unhealthy bodies. All spoken lines are pre-recorded clips (Rishi), with voice only after 🔊. Content: `catalogue/catalogue_games.json` → `ww089` (30 rounds, 3 levels).

**15. Accessibility.** Meaning is carried by the pictures, not by colour alone; labels are screen text for the parent only. No guilt language; Indian foods (ragi porridge, rice and dal) included. Plus: focus ring #F59E0B 8 px plus glow, targets at least 240 px, reduced-motion flag honoured, all audio optional, no reading required.

**16. Offline / failure.** Fully bundled; no network. If content fails to load or validate, the card is hidden and an error screen returns to the host.

**17. Parent options / data.** Enable/disable, starting level, reset. Stored locally: current level per game only. Reset clears it without touching time settings.

**18. Tests.** Validator: `validate_games.py` (schema, answer keys, 8-word prompts, Unicode 11 emoji, TTS rules). Each round has one defensible answer (plus `also_ok` where listed); choices distinct; labels match choices; `fade` is never a right answer. Plus host contract tests: launch blocked when disabled or out of budget; budget expiry in instructions, play, feedback and pause leads to a calm end; BACK in every state; cold start in airplane mode; a real-remote pass on the OnePlus TV.

**19. Evidence and limits.** Background: healthy eating and hygiene habits are part of early-years health goals (NCF-FS, NCERT 2022). Limitation: framework, not effect evidence; food messages are kept neutral (no shame, no 'bad food') on purpose. We claim only practice of the skill inside the activity.
