**1. Identity.** Original title "Sound Match", catalogue id WW-097. Source record unchanged in master.json. Decision: **deferred: needs variant pick with `sfx` clip (audio prompt)**.

**2. Age band.** Record 4-6; fits him.

**3. Objective (observable).** He hears a short real-world sound (a cow, a bell, a train) and picks the picture that makes it.

**4. What he practises.** Auditory discrimination and linking sounds to things.

**5. Originality and dedup.** No existing game uses a sound as the question. Sound Sequence (ww022) repeats tones; this names the source of a sound. Different. Decision: **deferred**, not merged.

**6. Why deferred.** The whole question is a sound. Voice lines play only after 🔊, so spoken onomatopoeia ("moo moo") cannot be the stimulus. A short non-speech sound effect is allowed, but `pick` rounds have no field to play one.

**7. Schema proposal (precise).** Extend `pick` rounds with two optional fields; no new variant name is needed:
```
{"id":"ww097-1-01","prompt":"Which one makes this sound?","say":"Listen. Which one makes this sound?",
  "sfx":"cow","sfx_plays":2,"board":["🔊"],"choices":["🐄","🐱","🐶"],"answer":0,"labels":["cow","cat","dog"],
  "hint":"Listen again. Is it big and deep or small and high?","why":"That was a cow. Moo!","scene":"🔊","fade":1}
```
- `sfx`: key into a bundled manifest `assets/sfx/manifest.json` (ogg, 0.5-2 s, no speech); the validator checks that the key exists and that the clip has no voice flag.
- `sfx_plays`: how many times it auto-plays after the first focus (1-3); the board speaker tile replays it on OK (always allowed, it is not voice).
- Engine: board 🔊 tile focusable (the first focus), OK plays the clip, LEFT/RIGHT over choices; 🔊 in the top bar still only controls spoken lines.
- Needed clips (about 20): cow, dog, cat, duck, rooster, elephant, lion, bird, bell, drum, horn, train, bus, rain, wind, clap, water, phone, doorbell, laugh.
- Validator: `sfx` must be in the manifest; every choice has a label; no `sfx` on rounds that rely on `say` alone.
