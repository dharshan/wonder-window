# WW-081 · Spot the Mistake (our id `ww081`)
Status: **design_spec_draft** (awaiting father/reviewer approval) · Category: H. Critical Thinking & Decision-Making / Identifying Mistakes · Variant: `pick`

**1. Identity.** Original title "Spot the Mistake", catalogue id WW-081. Source record unchanged in master.json.

**2. Age band.** Record 5-7; fits him. L1 is easy for him, L3 (pairs that do not match, one counting round) is a stretch.

**3. Objective (observable).** He finds the one thing that does not belong in a described scene or pair (a swimsuit in the rain, a fish in a tree, a ship that sails on a tree).

**4. What he practises.** Noticing when something does not fit what he knows about the world; checking a picture against his own knowledge; saying why. (Source objective: "Find what is wrong or out of place in a scene".)

**5. Originality and dedup.** Nearest: Pattern Detective's 'Which one does not belong?' (merged mode pattern-odd-one-out) finds the odd one by category. Here the mistake is a real-world contradiction in a scene (weather, place, time, use), and L3 checks pairs. Different objective. Decision: **own game** (reviewer to confirm it is not too close to odd-one-out).

**6. Core loop.** A short scene is named and shown with 1-2 picture clues; 3-4 things in it are offered; one is the mistake. At L3 three pairs are shown (cow and milk, sheep and cake...) and he picks the half of the pair that does not fit.
Example rounds:
- L1 `ww081-1-01`: "Which one is the mistake?", board 🌧️, choices 👙 ☂️ 👢, answer 👙. Spoken: "It is raining. Cherry has an umbrella, boots, and a swimsuit. Which one is the mistake?"
- L2 `ww081-2-01`: "Which one is the mistake?", board 🌙, choices 🕶️ 🦷 🛏️ 🧸, answer 🕶️. Spoken: "It is bedtime. Cherry has a teddy, a bed, clean teeth, and sunglasses. Which one is the mistake?"
- L3 `ww081-3-01`: "Which pair is a mistake?", board 🐄 🥛 🐔 🥚 🐑 🍰, choices 🥚 🍰 🥛, answer 🍰. Spoken: "Cow and milk. Hen and egg. Sheep and cake. Which pair is the mistake?"

**7. Child-facing instruction.** Visual demo by Cherry in round 1 of the first session. Narration only on 🔊: "Cherry made a picture, but a silly mistake is hiding in it. Can you spot the mistake?" Prompt text 8 words or fewer (he can't read; it is for the parent).

**8. Remote mapping.** LEFT/RIGHT over the choices, OK confirms, UP 🔊, BACK pause. Initial focus: 🔊 (pulsing) on the first round, then the first choice. Focus never lands on an inert element; exactly one element is focused.

**9. Difficulty.** L1 3 choices, very obvious mistakes. L2 4 choices, the mistake needs the weather, place or time. L3 three pairs, the mistake is one pair (one counting round). Levels: L1 Silly things 🙈; L2 Where it belongs 🧩; L3 Pairs that match 🔗.

**10. Hint / retry / recovery.** After 1 miss the choice fades and the `hint` is shown (voice only on 🔊). After 2 misses the answer is shown and he presses it ("You kept going!"). 🔊 always replays the line. Nothing is lost.

**11. Success, session end, early exit.** Session = 5 rounds, then the end_lines and the Play again / Back to games buttons. Early exit any time via BACK: it cancels any local action first, then opens the pause sheet; a second BACK returns to the host. No confirmation is needed because no progress is lost.

**12. Feedback.** Matches the record ("Gentle positive visual confirmation on success. Neutral, non-punitive feedback on errors with option to try again. No timers or pressure."). Correct: one of 6 `cheers`, the scene fills (🔍), soft `correct` sound. Miss: neutral wobble plus `tryagain` taps; never red, never "wrong".

**13. Session time.** About 20-35 s per round; 5 rounds take about 2-3 min. Target 1.5-4 min (record estimate 1.5-3.5 min).

**14. Assets / audio / content.** Large emoji choice cards, scene clue on the board, gentle 'aha' sparkle. All spoken lines are pre-recorded clips (Rishi), with voice only after 🔊. Content: `catalogue/catalogue_games.json` → `ww081` (30 rounds, 3 levels).

**15. Accessibility.** Meaning is carried by the pictures, not by colour alone; labels are screen text for the parent only.  Plus: focus ring #F59E0B 8 px plus glow, targets at least 240 px, reduced-motion flag honoured, all audio optional, no reading required.

**16. Offline / failure.** Fully bundled; no network. If content fails to load or validate, the card is hidden and an error screen returns to the host.

**17. Parent options / data.** Enable/disable, starting level, reset. Stored locally: current level per game only. Reset clears it without touching time settings.

**18. Tests.** Validator: `validate_games.py` (schema, answer keys, 8-word prompts, Unicode 11 emoji, TTS rules). Each round has one defensible answer (plus `also_ok` where listed); choices distinct; labels match choices; `fade` is never a right answer. Plus host contract tests: launch blocked when disabled or out of budget; budget expiry in instructions, play, feedback and pause leads to a calm end; BACK in every state; cold start in airplane mode; a real-remote pass on the OnePlus TV.

**19. Evidence and limits.** Background only: error detection is a typical early reasoning-and-language skill (NCF-FS, NCERT 2022, reasoning goals). Limitation: framework, not effect evidence; we have no evidence that it transfers. We claim only practice of the skill inside the activity.
