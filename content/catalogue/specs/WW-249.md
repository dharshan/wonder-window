# WW-249 · Science Story
Status: **merged_into lab (mode `lab-sky`)** · Category: F. Science & Discovery / Space Basics · Source age 9-10 · Variant: `pick`

**Objective (source).** Learn simple facts about the sun, moon, stars, and day/night

**Decision.** merged_into `lab` (Wonder Lab, core game) as the new mode **Sun, Moon and Stars** (`lab-sky`, 30 rounds, 3 levels, 3 rounds per session, `pick`). Alias WW-249 -> lab recorded in the mode.

**Reason.** Wonder Lab level 4 ('Body and sky') already has a few sky questions (daylight, our planet, the closest star) and Time of Day (ww037) covers the parts of the day, but sun, moon, stars and day/night have no set of their own. Same action as Wonder Lab (pick the answer), so it is content for the core game, not a new game. Source age 9-10: L1 (day or night, warm sun, stars) fits him; L3 is a stretch (rocket, telescope, nocturnal animals, 'the moon goes around the Earth').

**How it plays.** A question about the sky; 2-3 big picture choices; one fact in the `why` line. Hint after a miss; nothing is lost. Example rounds:
- L1 `ww249-1-01`: "Is it day or night?", board 🌞, choices 🌞 🌙.
- L2 `ww249-2-04`: "Which one changes shape?", board 🌑 🌒 🌓 🌔 🌕, choices 🌙 🌞 ⭐.
- L3 `ww249-3-03`: "What shows far stars?", choices 🔭 👓 🔦.

**Evidence and limits.** Sky and day/night are early-years science themes (NCF-FS, NCERT 2022); framework, not effect evidence. 'Never look at the sun' is taught in L3.
