# WW-125 · Money Match (our id `ww125`)
Status: **design_spec_draft** (awaiting father/reviewer approval) · Category: J. Real-World Knowledge & Life Skills / Money Basics · Variant: `pick`

**1. Identity.** Original title "Money Match", catalogue id WW-125. Source record unchanged in master.json.

**2. Age band.** Record 7-9; this is above his age. L1 (counting one-rupee coins up to five) fits him; L2 and L3 are a stretch and are for later (a parent can leave the starting level at 1).

**3. Objective (observable).** He picks the coins that add up to a price (L1 one-rupee coins, L2 one, two and five rupee coins), then finds the item whose price matches a handful of coins (L3).

**4. What he practises.** Counting coins; adding small amounts; linking a number to the money it stands for. (Source objective: "Match coins or simple amounts to prices of small items".)

**5. Originality and dedup.** Make Ten (ww052) and Count On (mode) add numbers, but nothing uses money. Same pick action; the objective (coins and prices) is new. Decision: **own game**. Coins are drawn as text chips (`₹1 ₹2 ₹5`) because the coin emoji is newer than Unicode 11.

**6. Core loop.** A treat and its price sit on the board. He picks the coin group that pays exactly. L3 shows his coins on the board and he picks the item with that price. Rupees only; no real shops, no payment words.
Example rounds:
- L1 `ww125-1-01`: "Pay for it with coins!", board 🍬 ₹2, choices ₹1 ₹1 ₹1 ₹1 ₹1 ₹1, answer ₹1 ₹1. Spoken: "The toffee costs two rupees. Each coin is one rupee. Which coins make two rupees?"
- L2 `ww125-2-01`: "Which coins make the price?", board 🍬 ₹3, choices ₹1 ₹1 ₹2 ₹2 ₹2 ₹1, answer ₹2 ₹1. Spoken: "The toffee costs three rupees. Which coins make three rupees?"
- L3 `ww125-3-01`: "Which costs exactly this much?", board ₹5, choices 🍦 ₹8 🍪 ₹5 🍬 ₹2, answer 🍪 ₹5. Spoken: "You have five rupees. That is five rupees. Which one costs exactly five rupees?"

**7. Child-facing instruction.** Visual demo by the buddy (Pip) in round 1 of the first session. Narration only on 🔊: "the buddy (Pip) is playing shop. Each treat has a price. Help her find the right coins!" Prompt text 8 words or fewer (he can't read; it is for the parent).

**8. Remote mapping.** LEFT/RIGHT over the choices, OK confirms, UP 🔊, BACK pause. Initial focus: 🔊 (pulsing) on the first round, then the first choice. Focus never lands on an inert element; exactly one element is focused.

**9. Difficulty.** L1 two to five one-rupee coins, three choices that differ by one coin. L2 combinations of one, two and five rupee coins to prices from two to ten; some prices have two right answers (`also_ok`). L3 coins of one to ten rupees, prices up to twenty. Levels: L1 One rupee coins 💰; L2 Different coins 🔢; L3 Which costs this? 🛒.

**10. Hint / retry / recovery.** After 1 miss the choice fades and the `hint` is shown (voice only on 🔊). After 2 misses the answer is shown and he presses it ("You kept going!"). 🔊 always replays the line. Nothing is lost.

**11. Success, session end, early exit.** Session = 5 rounds, then the end_lines and the Play again / Back to games buttons. Early exit any time via BACK: it cancels any local action first, then opens the pause sheet; a second BACK returns to the host. No confirmation is needed because no progress is lost.

**12. Feedback.** Matches the record ("Soft positive confirmation. Errors receive neutral feedback and the chance to try again. No negative sounds or messages."). Correct: one of 6 `cheers`, the scene fills (💰), soft `correct` sound. Miss: neutral wobble plus `tryagain` taps; never red, never "wrong".

**13. Session time.** About 20-35 s per round; 5 rounds take about 2-3 min. Target 1.5-4 min (record estimate 1.5-4 min).

**14. Assets / audio / content.** Candy-card choices with text coin chips (screen text) and a treat emoji; a soft 'clink' correct sound. All spoken lines are pre-recorded clips (Rishi), with voice only after 🔊. Content: `catalogue/catalogue_games.json` → `ww125` (30 rounds, 3 levels).

**15. Accessibility.** Meaning is carried by the pictures, not by colour alone; labels are screen text for the parent only. Spoken lines use number words ('five rupees'), never the symbol. Long coin chips may wrap onto two lines; the card must grow, not shrink the text. Plus: focus ring #F59E0B 8 px plus glow, targets at least 240 px, reduced-motion flag honoured, all audio optional, no reading required.

**16. Offline / failure.** Fully bundled; no network. If content fails to load or validate, the card is hidden and an error screen returns to the host.

**17. Parent options / data.** Enable/disable, starting level, reset. Stored locally: current level per game only. Reset clears it without touching time settings.

**18. Tests.** Validator: `validate_games.py` (schema, answer keys, 8-word prompts, Unicode 11 emoji, TTS rules). Each round has one defensible answer (plus `also_ok` where listed); choices distinct; labels match choices; `fade` is never a right answer. Plus host contract tests: launch blocked when disabled or out of budget; budget expiry in instructions, play, feedback and pause leads to a calm end; BACK in every state; cold start in airplane mode; a real-remote pass on the OnePlus TV.

**19. Evidence and limits.** Background: counting and adding with coins is part of early numeracy and everyday-money goals (NCF-FS, NCERT 2022, numeracy). Limitation: framework, not effect evidence; the record is for 7-9 year olds, so only L1 is claimed for him. We claim only practice of the skill inside the activity.
