# WW-091 · Care for Nature (our id `ww091`)
Status: **design_spec_draft** (awaiting father/reviewer approval) · Category: J. Real-World Knowledge & Life Skills / Environmental Care · Variant: `pick`

**1. Identity.** Original title "Care for Nature", catalogue id WW-091. Source record unchanged in master.json.

**2. Age band.** Record 5-7; fits him (L3 is a stretch).

**3. Objective (observable).** He picks the action that helps plants, animals or the environment (water the plant, bin the wrapper, save water, plant a seed).

**4. What he practises.** Environmental care and consequences: how small everyday acts help plants, animals and clean places. (Source objective: "Choose actions that help take care of plants, animals, or the environment".)

**5. Originality and dedup.** Plant Helper (ww034) is about what plants need and how they grow; this game is about our actions towards the wider environment (rubbish, water, energy, animals). Different objective. Decision: **own game**.

**6. Core loop.** A short situation (a thirsty plant, a banana peel, a dripping tap) and 2-4 actions; he picks the caring one. L3 includes a round with three caring switches (`also_ok`).
Example rounds:
- L1 `ww091-1-01`: "What helps nature?", board 🌱, choices 👟 💧, answer 💧. Spoken: "The little plant is thirsty. What helps nature? Giving it water, or stepping on it?"
- L2 `ww091-2-01`: "What helps nature?", board 🦷, choices 🛁 💦 🚰, answer 🚰. Spoken: "We brush our teeth. What helps nature? Turning off the tap, letting it run, or filling the bath?"
- L3 `ww091-3-01`: "What helps nature?", board 🥫, choices 🌊 🌷 🛣️ 🏖️, answer 🌷. Spoken: "We have an empty tin. What helps nature? Planting a flower in it, throwing it in the lake, burying it in the sand, or leaving it on the road?"

**7. Child-facing instruction.** Visual demo by the buddy (Pip) in round 1 of the first session. Narration only on 🔊: "the buddy (Pip) loves nature. Plants, birds, and animals need our care. Let's choose what helps!" Prompt text 8 words or fewer (he can't read; it is for the parent).

**8. Remote mapping.** LEFT/RIGHT over the choices, OK confirms, UP 🔊, BACK pause. Initial focus: 🔊 (pulsing) on the first round, then the first choice. Focus never lands on an inert element; exactly one element is focused.

**9. Difficulty.** L1 two choices, obvious. L2 three choices, everyday habits (tap, bag, bottle, paper). L3 four choices, reuse and reduce ideas. Levels: L1 Little helpers 🌱; L2 Everyday care 💧; L3 Big ideas 🌳.

**10. Hint / retry / recovery.** After 1 miss the choice fades and the `hint` is shown (voice only on 🔊). After 2 misses the answer is shown and he presses it ("You kept going!"). 🔊 always replays the line. Nothing is lost.

**11. Success, session end, early exit.** Session = 5 rounds, then the end_lines and the Play again / Back to games buttons. Early exit any time via BACK: it cancels any local action first, then opens the pause sheet; a second BACK returns to the host. No confirmation is needed because no progress is lost.

**12. Feedback.** Matches the record ("Gentle positive visual confirmation on success. Neutral, non-punitive feedback on errors with option to try again. No timers or pressure."). Correct: one of 6 `cheers`, the scene fills (🌍), soft `correct` sound. Miss: neutral wobble plus `tryagain` taps; never red, never "wrong".

**13. Session time.** About 20-35 s per round; 5 rounds take about 2-3 min. Target 1.5-4 min (record estimate 1.5-3.5 min).

**14. Assets / audio / content.** Candy-card choices; no scary or sad pictures of harmed animals. All spoken lines are pre-recorded clips (Rishi), with voice only after 🔊. Content: `catalogue/catalogue_games.json` → `ww091` (30 rounds, 3 levels).

**15. Accessibility.** Meaning is carried by the pictures, not by colour alone; labels are screen text for the parent only. Every round that involves an animal says 'with a grown-up' or avoids touching wild animals. Plus: focus ring #F59E0B 8 px plus glow, targets at least 240 px, reduced-motion flag honoured, all audio optional, no reading required.

**16. Offline / failure.** Fully bundled; no network. If content fails to load or validate, the card is hidden and an error screen returns to the host.

**17. Parent options / data.** Enable/disable, starting level, reset. Stored locally: current level per game only. Reset clears it without touching time settings.

**18. Tests.** Validator: `validate_games.py` (schema, answer keys, 8-word prompts, Unicode 11 emoji, TTS rules). Each round has one defensible answer (plus `also_ok` where listed); choices distinct; labels match choices; `fade` is never a right answer. Plus host contract tests: launch blocked when disabled or out of budget; budget expiry in instructions, play, feedback and pause leads to a calm end; BACK in every state; cold start in airplane mode; a real-remote pass on the OnePlus TV.

**19. Evidence and limits.** Background: care for the environment and living things is an early-years value goal (NCF-FS, NCERT 2022). Limitation: framework, not effect evidence; actions are chosen only in the pretend scenario. We claim only practice of the skill inside the activity.
