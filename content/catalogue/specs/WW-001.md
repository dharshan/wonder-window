**Objective (source).** Sort objects by a single visual attribute (colour)
**Mechanics (source).** Large coloured nests are displayed. A row of coloured shapes appears at the bottom. Child uses LEFT/RIGHT to highlight a nest, UP/DOWN to cycle shapes, OK to place the selected shape into the highlighted nest.
