**6. Why deferred.** Existing variants cannot place a part at a free position (`place` only drops items into bins, `mirror` fills a mirrored grid). Faking it with pick rounds would not be building.

**7. Schema proposal (precise).** New variant `compose`, round fields:
```
{"id":"ww133-1-01","variant":"compose","mode":"build","prompt":"Build the little house!",
  "say":"Look at the house. Build one just like it! Move, then press OK.",
  "rows":3,"cols":3,"parts":["🟫","🧱","🔺"],
  "target":[[null,"🔺",null],["🧱","🧱","🧱"],["🧱","🟫","🧱"]],
  "min_parts":0,"hint":"Start at the bottom row.","why":"Your house is built!","scene":"🏠"}
```
- `mode` `"build"`: grid `rows` 2-4 by `cols` 2-4; `parts` 2-6 distinct emoji (tray); `target` is the picture to copy (each cell a part or null) or `null` for free build; `min_parts` is how many parts a free build needs before 'Done' (1-6). Free build is the last level only.
- Remote: UP/DOWN between the tray row and the grid rows; LEFT/RIGHT inside the row; OK on a tray part selects it, OK on a grid cell places it (OK on a placed cell removes it); BACK = undo the last placement first, then the pause sheet. A big 'Done' button appears when the target matches (copy mode: it finishes by itself) or `min_parts` is reached (free mode).
- Never wrong: a part in a different cell stays; a copy round finishes when the grid equals the target; the hint highlights the next empty target cell after 2 idle presses.
- Validator: every non-null target cell is in `parts`; target uses every part at least once; `rows`/`cols` within range; free mode needs `min_parts`.
- Engine cost: one new screen (tray plus grid, cursor), about the size of the `mirror` variant.
