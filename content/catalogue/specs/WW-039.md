# WW-039 · Letter Sound Match (mode in core `word`)
Status: **design_spec_draft** (awaiting father/reviewer approval) · Category: D. Language & Literacy / Phonics / Letter-Sound · Variant: `pick`

**1. Identity.** Original title "Letter Sound Match", catalogue id WW-039. Source record unchanged in master.json. Decision: **merged_into word (mode `word-letter-sound`)**.

**2. Age band.** Record says 5-6 primary / 4-6, 5-7. He is 5½: fits from L1.

**3. Objective (observable).** He matches a letter to its sound by picking a picture that starts with that sound, and a picture to its letter. (Source objective: "Match a letter to the sound it makes or to a picture that starts with that sound".)

**4. What he practises.** Letter-sound links; first sounds of words.

**5. Originality and dedup.** Word Builder (core `word`) already has 'Which one starts like...?' and 'Which letter makes the kuh sound?'. Same objective and same action. Letter Garden (WW-004) matches letter shapes only. Decision: **merged_into word**; mode with 30 rounds. Spoken lines use sounds (buh, mmm), never letter names.

**6. Core loop.** One round at a time, 3 levels of 10 rounds (30 total), 5 rounds per set (3 for a mode). Correct answer: a cheer, the scene fills, soft sound. Wrong: neutral wobble and a hint; no penalty.
Example rounds:
- L1 `ww039-1-01`: "Which one starts with buh?" Spoken: "This letter says buh. Which one starts with buh? Bear, watch, or rabbit?"
- L3 `ww039-3-01`: "Which letter says it?" Spoken: "Sun starts with sss. Which letter says sss?"

**7. Child-facing instruction.** Visual demo by Cherry in round 1 of the first set. Narration only on 🔊: "(shares the voice of core `word`; round lines only)" Prompt text 8 words or fewer (he can't read; it is for the parent).

**8. Remote mapping.** LEFT/RIGHT (and UP/DOWN on the 2x2 grid) over the choices, OK answers, UP on the speaker for 🔊. BACK pauses (Keep playing / Back to games). Initial focus: 🔊 (pulsing) on the first round, then the first choice. Focus never lands on an inert element; exactly one element is focused.

**9. Difficulty.** L1 letter to picture with unlike sounds, L2 with near sounds (buh, duh, guh), L3 picture to letter. In-set rule: 3 first-try in a row moves up one level, at most once per set; 2 misses in a row moves down silently. Levels: L1 Sound to picture 🔊; L2 Near sounds 👂; L3 Picture to letter 🔤.

**10. Hint / retry / recovery.** After 1 miss: the hint line plays (on 🔊) and one wrong choice fades. After 2 misses: the answer pulses and he presses it ("You kept going!"). 🔊 always replays the line. Nothing is lost.

**11. Success, session end, early exit.** 5 rounds per set, then the end_lines and Play again / Back to games. BACK cancels any local action first, then opens the pause sheet; a second BACK returns to the host. No confirmation is needed because no progress is lost.

**12. Feedback.** Correct: one of 6 `cheers`, the scene fills, soft `correct` sound. Miss: neutral wobble plus `tryagain` taps; never red, never "wrong". Record says: "Soft positive confirmation. Option to hear sound and word again."

**13. Session time.** About 20-40 s per round; a set of 5 rounds takes about 2-3 min. Target 1.5-4 min.

**14. Assets / audio / content.** Emoji pictures (Unicode 11 or older, no ZWJ) in white candy cards, Cherry the sparrow, soft sfx. All spoken lines are pre-recorded clips (Rishi), voice only after 🔊; sounds are written as buh, mmm, sss, never letter names. Content: `catalogue/batches/b2.json` → merged mode `word-letter-sound`.

**15. Accessibility.** Meaning never depends on colour alone; large cards. Focus ring #F59E0B 8 px plus glow, targets at least 240 px, reduced-motion flag honoured, all audio optional, no reading required (labels are for the parent).

**16. Offline / failure.** Fully bundled; no network. If content fails to load or validate, the card is hidden and an error screen returns to the host.

**17. Parent options / data.** Enable/disable, rounds per set (4 or 5), reset progress. Stored locally: current level per game only. Reset clears it without touching time settings.

**18. Tests.** Validator `validate_games.py` (schema, answer keys, 8-word prompts, Unicode 11 emoji, TTS rules) passes. Each round has one defensible answer; choices are distinct; spoken lines contain no isolated letters. Plus host contract tests: launch blocked when disabled or out of budget; budget expiry in every state leads to a calm end; BACK in every state; cold start in airplane mode; a real-remote pass on the OnePlus TV.

**19. Evidence and limits.** Phonics and letter-sound knowledge: NCERT NCF-FS (2022). Limitation: framework only. We claim only practice of the skill inside the activity.
