# WW-184 · Sky Friends (our id `ww184`)
Status: **design_spec_draft** (awaiting father/reviewer approval) · Category: F. Science & Discovery / Space Basics · Variant: `pick`

**1. Identity.** Original title "Observe Story", catalogue id WW-184. Source record unchanged in master.json.

**2. Age band.** Record 4-6; fits him exactly.

**3. Objective (observable).** He picks what shines in the sky in the day and at night (sun, moon, stars), then simple moon shapes and why we have day and night.

**4. What he practises.** Basic sky facts: the sun in the day, the moon and stars at night, the moon changes shape, the Earth turns; a base for later science. (Source objective: "Learn simple facts about the sun, moon, stars, and day/night".)

**5. Originality and dedup.** Time of Day (ww037) matches activities to morning, afternoon or night; Weather Watch is about weather. Neither asks about the sun, moon or stars. Same pick action, new objective. WW-441 (cluster WW-DUP-042) is the same. Decision: **own game**.

**6. Core loop.** A sky picture on the board; he picks the sun, moon, star or time that fits. L2 names moon shapes and sunset. L3 asks 'why?' with silly distractors.
Example rounds:
- L1 `ww184-1-01`: "Day or night in the sky?", board 🌃, choices 🍕 🌙 🐟, answer 🌙. Spoken: "It is night. What shines in the night sky? The moon, a fish, or a pizza?"
- L2 `ww184-2-01`: "What is in the sky?", board 🌙, choices 🌑 🌓 🌕, answer 🌕. Spoken: "Which moon is a big round circle?"
- L3 `ww184-3-01`: "Think about the sky.", board 🌞 ⭐, choices 🛏️ ☀️ 🛒, answer ☀️. Spoken: "Stars are there all day. Why can we not see them? The sun is too bright, the stars go to bed, or the stars go shopping?"

**7. Child-facing instruction.** Visual demo by Cherry in round 1 of the first session. Narration only on 🔊: "Cherry loves the sky. There is a sun, a moon, and lots of stars. Can you help Cherry?" Prompt text 8 words or fewer (he can't read; it is for the parent).

**8. Remote mapping.** LEFT/RIGHT over the choices, OK confirms, UP 🔊, BACK pause. Initial focus: 🔊 (pulsing) on the first round, then the first choice. Focus never lands on an inert element; exactly one element is focused.

**9. Difficulty.** L1 day or night with obvious pictures. L2 moon shapes (full, half, dark, thin), sunset, shadow, night animals. L3 reasons (the sun is a star, Earth turns, moon is lit by the sun), kept to one short sentence each. Levels: L1 Day or night 🌞; L2 Moon, sun, stars 🌙; L3 Why is it so? ⭐.

**10. Hint / retry / recovery.** After 1 miss the choice fades and the `hint` is shown (voice only on 🔊). After 2 misses the answer is shown and he presses it ("You kept going!"). 🔊 always replays the line. Nothing is lost.

**11. Success, session end, early exit.** Session = 5 rounds, then the end_lines and the Play again / Back to games buttons. Early exit any time via BACK: it cancels any local action first, then opens the pause sheet; a second BACK returns to the host. No confirmation is needed because no progress is lost.

**12. Feedback.** Matches the record ("Gentle, positive visual feedback on success. Neutral and constructive response to errors with opportunity to try again. No pressure, timers, or negative messaging."). Correct: one of 6 `cheers`, the scene fills (🌤️), soft `correct` sound. Miss: neutral wobble plus `tryagain` taps; never red, never "wrong".

**13. Session time.** About 20-35 s per round; 5 rounds take about 2-3 min. Target 1.5-4 min (record estimate 1.5-4 min).

**14. Assets / audio / content.** Sky emoji (🌙 🌕 🌓 🌑 🌃 🌞 🌅 🌇 ⭐), candy-card choices. All spoken lines are pre-recorded clips (Rishi), with voice only after 🔊. Content: `catalogue/catalogue_games.json` → `ww184` (30 rounds, 3 levels).

**15. Accessibility.** Meaning is carried by the pictures, not by colour alone; labels are screen text for the parent only.  Plus: focus ring #F59E0B 8 px plus glow, targets at least 240 px, reduced-motion flag honoured, all audio optional, no reading required.

**16. Offline / failure.** Fully bundled; no network. If content fails to load or validate, the card is hidden and an error screen returns to the host.

**17. Parent options / data.** Enable/disable, starting level, reset. Stored locally: current level per game only. Reset clears it without touching time settings.

**18. Tests.** Validator: `validate_games.py` (schema, answer keys, 8-word prompts, Unicode 11 emoji, TTS rules). Each round has one defensible answer (plus `also_ok` where listed); choices distinct; labels match choices; `fade` is never a right answer. Plus host contract tests: launch blocked when disabled or out of budget; budget expiry in instructions, play, feedback and pause leads to a calm end; BACK in every state; cold start in airplane mode; a real-remote pass on the OnePlus TV.

**19. Evidence and limits.** Background: space basics and day and night are NCF-FS (NCERT, 2022) environmental-awareness goals. Limitation: framework, not effect evidence. Facts reviewed: the sun is a star, the moon shines by sunlight, the Earth turns once a day. We claim only practice of the skill inside the activity.
