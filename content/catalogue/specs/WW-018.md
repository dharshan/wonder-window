# WW-018 · Weather Watch (our id `ww018`)
Status: **design_spec_draft** (awaiting father/reviewer approval) · Category: F. Science & Discovery / Weather & Seasons · Variant: `pick`

**1. Identity.** Original title "Weather Watch", catalogue id WW-018. Source record unchanged in master.json.

**2. Age band.** Record 4-6 / 4-7; fits him.

**3. Objective (observable).** Given a weather picture, he chooses clothing or an activity that suits it (more than one can be right at L3).

**4. What he practises.** Linking weather to everyday choices; Bangalore seasons (hot April, June-October rains, cool December mornings) and far-away snow. (Source objective: "Match weather conditions to appropriate clothing or activities".)

**5. Originality and dedup.** Wonder Lab has 4 weather rounds as part of science; here weather to clothing is the whole objective. Keep separate, and drop lab-3-10/11 overlap when Lab is next revised. Decision: **own game**.

**6. Core loop.** Weather window; 3-4 clothing/activity cards with labels; the chosen item is put on Cherry.
Example rounds:
- L1 `ww018-1-01`: "What do we need?", board 🌧️; choices ☂️ 🧤 🕶️; answer ☂️. Spoken: "It is raining. What do we need? Umbrella, gloves, or sunglasses?"
- L2 `ww018-2-01`: "What do we need?", board ☀️; choices 🏊 🧣 🧢 ⛄; answer 🏊. Spoken: "A hot sunny day. Which one is best? Swim, scarf, cap, or make a snowman?"
- L3 `ww018-3-01`: "What do we need?", board 🌦️; choices ☂️ 🧢 ⛄ 🧤; answer ☂️. Spoken: "Sunny now, but clouds coming. What could help? Umbrella, cap, make a snowman, or gloves?"

**7. Child-facing instruction.** Visual demo by Cherry in round 1 of the first session. Narration only on 🔊: "Let's look at the weather, and choose what helps!" Prompt text 8 words or fewer (he can't read; it is for the parent).

**8. Remote mapping.** Row of 3-4 cards, LEFT/RIGHT, OK, UP 🔊, BACK pause. Initial focus: 🔊 (pulsing) on the first round, then the first choice. Focus never lands on an inert element; exactly one element is focused.

**9. Difficulty.** L1 clear matches, L2 4 options and activities, L3 multiple acceptable answers (also_ok). Levels: L1 Easy weather 🌤️, L2 More choices 🌦️, L3 More than one is good 🌈.

**10. Hint / retry / recovery.** Miss: 'Look at the weather. Is it rainy, sunny or cold?', and the weather animates. 🔊 always replays the line. After the second miss the answer is shown and he presses it ("You kept going!"). Nothing is lost.

**11. Success, session end, early exit.** 5 rounds. Each set is 5 rounds. BACK cancels any local action first (palette, peek), then opens the pause sheet; a second BACK returns to the host. No confirmation is needed because no progress is lost.

**12. Feedback.** Matches the record ("Character happily prepares with soft animation. Neutral feedback on less suitable choices."). Correct: one of 6 `cheers`, the scene fills (🌦️), soft `correct` sound. Miss: neutral wobble plus `tryagain` taps; never red, never "wrong".

**13. Session time.** About 15-25 s per round; about 2 min. Target 1.5-4 min (record estimate 1.5-3 min).

**14. Assets / audio / content.** Weather emoji in a window frame, Cherry dress-up overlay (item emoji placed on the buddy). All spoken lines are pre-recorded clips (Rishi), with voice only after 🔊. Content: `catalogue/catalogue_games.json` → `ww018`.

**15. Accessibility.** Labels on cards; weather shown with icons and animation, never colour only. Plus: focus ring #F59E0B 8 px plus glow, targets at least 240 px, reduced-motion flag honoured, all audio optional, no reading required.

**16. Offline / failure.** Fully bundled; no network. If content fails to load or validate, the card is hidden and an error screen returns to the host.

**17. Parent options / data.** Enable/disable, reset. Stored locally: current level per game only. Reset clears it without touching time settings.

**18. Tests.** Validator: `validate_games.py` (schema, answer keys, 8-word prompts, Unicode 11 emoji, TTS rules). also_ok items valid; snow rounds framed as 'far away in the mountains' (no snow in Bangalore).
