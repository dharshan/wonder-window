# WW-008 · What Comes Next? (our id `ww008`)
Status: **design_spec_draft** (awaiting father/reviewer approval) · Category: H. Critical Thinking & Decision-Making / Prediction & Sequencing · Variant: `pick`

**1. Identity.** Original title "What Comes Next?", catalogue id WW-008, legacy alias A005. Source record unchanged in master.json.

**2. Age band.** Record 5-7; fits him now.

**3. Objective (observable).** Given two picture steps of an everyday routine, he picks the picture that logically comes next.

**4. What he practises.** Everyday cause and order (getting ready, cooking, tidying, shopping); in L3, rejecting a choice that belongs earlier in the story. (Source objective: "Predict the logical next step in a simple everyday sequence".)

**5. Originality and dedup.** Wonder Lab is science cause and effect (melting, growing); Story Steps (WW-017) orders all panels (an order action). This one picks the ending for daily-life routines: different objective content and different action from Story Steps. Legacy A005 has the same title and only a concept, so it is kept as an alias. Decision: **own game**.

**6. Core loop.** Two panels with arrows and a ❓ panel; 3 ending cards. The correct card slides into the ❓ and the mini story plays.
Example rounds:
- L1 `ww008-1-01`: "What happens next?", board 👕 ➡️ 👟 ➡️ ❓; choices 🛁 🛏️ 🏫; answer 🏫. Spoken: "Put on your uniform. Put on your shoes. What happens next?"
- L2 `ww008-2-01`: "What happens next?", board ✉️ ➡️ 📮 ➡️ ❓; choices 📬 🎂 ✏️; answer 📬. Spoken: "We write a letter. We post it in the box. What happens next?"
- L3 `ww008-3-01`: "What happens next?", board 🥚 ➡️ 🍳 ➡️ ❓; choices 🐣 🍽️ 🥚; answer 🍽️. Spoken: "An egg. We cook it in the pan. What happens next?"

**7. Child-facing instruction.** Visual demo by the buddy (Pip) in round 1 of the first session. Narration only on 🔊: "Let's look at little picture stories from our day. You choose what happens next!" Prompt text 8 words or fewer (he can't read; it is for the parent).

**8. Remote mapping.** Row of 3 cards, LEFT/RIGHT, OK, UP for 🔊, BACK for pause. Initial focus: 🔊 (pulsing) on the first round, then the first choice. Focus never lands on an inert element; exactly one element is focused.

**9. Difficulty.** L1 familiar routines, L2 less obvious, L3 distractors that are earlier steps (an egg after cooking). Levels: L1 Everyday stories 🏠, L2 A bit trickier 🧩, L3 Think carefully 🤔.

**10. Hint / retry / recovery.** Miss: the two panels replay with a soft 'then...?'. 🔊 always replays the line. After the second miss the answer is shown and he presses it ("You kept going!"). Nothing is lost.

**11. Success, session end, early exit.** 5 stories per set. Each set is 5 rounds. BACK cancels any local action first (palette, peek), then opens the pause sheet; a second BACK returns to the host. No confirmation is needed because no progress is lost.

**12. Feedback.** Matches the record ("Gentle confirmation of the completed story. Neutral feedback + option to rethink on less logical choices."). Correct: one of 6 `cheers`, the scene fills (🎞️), soft `correct` sound. Miss: neutral wobble plus `tryagain` taps; never red, never "wrong".

**13. Session time.** About 20-30 s per round; about 2-2.5 min. Target 1.5-4 min (record estimate 1.5-3 min).

**14. Assets / audio / content.** Emoji panels on story cards, arrow connectors, and the slide-in animation. All spoken lines are pre-recorded clips (Rishi), with voice only after 🔊. Content: `catalogue/catalogue_games.json` → `ww008`.

**15. Accessibility.** Story text is optional voice; the pictures carry the meaning. Large cards. Plus: focus ring #F59E0B 8 px plus glow, targets at least 240 px, reduced-motion flag honoured, all audio optional, no reading required.

**16. Offline / failure.** Fully bundled; no network. If content fails to load or validate, the card is hidden and an error screen returns to the host.

**17. Parent options / data.** Enable/disable, reset. Stored locally: current level per game only. Reset clears it without touching time settings.

**18. Tests.** Validator: `validate_games.py` (schema, answer keys, 8-word prompts, Unicode 11 emoji, TTS rules). Every sequence has exactly one logical ending; L3 distractor is an earlier step; content reviewed for local routines. Plus host contract tests: launch blocked when disabled or out of budget; budget expiry in instructions, play, feedback and pause leads to a calm end; BACK in every state; cold start in airplane mode; a real-remote pass on the OnePlus TV.

**19. Evidence and limits.** Sequencing and prediction are listed in NCF for the Foundational Stage (NCERT, 2022) curricular goals for cognitive development. Limitation: general framework, not effect evidence for this game. We claim only practice of the skill inside the activity.
