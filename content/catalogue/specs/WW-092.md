# WW-092 · Get Ready (our id `ww092`)
Status: **design_spec_draft** (awaiting father/reviewer approval) · Category: J. Real-World Knowledge & Life Skills / Daily Routines · Variant: `order`

**1. Identity.** Original title "Get Ready", catalogue id WW-092. Source record unchanged in master.json.

**2. Age band.** Record 4-6; fits him exactly (3-5 steps).

**3. Objective (observable).** He puts 3-5 steps of a daily routine (getting ready for school, bedtime, washing hands, coming home) into the correct order.

**4. What he practises.** Sequencing his own daily routine; first, then, last; planning ahead. (Source objective: "Put the steps of a daily routine (getting ready for school/bed) in the correct order".)

**5. Originality and dedup.** Story Steps (ww017) orders the pictures of a story or a life cycle; here every card is a step of his own day (routines only). The action is the same `order` mechanic, so this is the closest dedup call in the batch; we keep it as an own game because the content is entirely his routines and carries labels such as 'brush teeth'. Reviewer to confirm own game or fold into ww017. Decision: **own game**.

**6. Core loop.** Shuffled step cards below, numbered slots above. Correct next card flies to the next slot; at the end the routine plays with labels.
Example rounds:
- L1 `ww092-1-01`: "Put the steps in order!", order ⏰ 🦷 🥣. Spoken: "Getting ready for school. Which picture comes first?"
- L2 `ww092-2-01`: "Put the steps in order!", order ⏰ 🦷 🛁 👕. Spoken: "Getting ready for school. Which picture comes first?"
- L3 `ww092-3-01`: "Put the steps in order!", order ⏰ 🦷 🛁 👕 👟. Spoken: "A school morning. Which picture comes first?"

**7. Child-facing instruction.** Visual demo by Cherry in round 1 of the first session. Narration only on 🔊: "Cherry's day has lots of steps. Help her put them in order, one by one!" Prompt text 8 words or fewer (he can't read; it is for the parent).

**8. Remote mapping.** LEFT/RIGHT over the remaining cards, OK sends the card to the next slot, UP 🔊, BACK pause. Initial focus: 🔊 (pulsing) on the first round, then the first choice. Focus never lands on an inert element; exactly one element is focused.

**9. Difficulty.** L1 three steps, L2 four steps, L3 four to five steps (ten different routines per level, each with exactly one defensible order). Levels: L1 Three steps 🌱; L2 Four steps 🌿; L3 Five steps 🌳.

**10. Hint / retry / recovery.** After 2 wrong picks the first missing step's card bounces and the voice gives the start (voice only on 🔊). After the second miss the answer is shown and he presses it ("You kept going!"). Nothing is lost.

**11. Success, session end, early exit.** Session = 5 rounds, then the end_lines and the Play again / Back to games buttons. Early exit any time via BACK: it cancels any local action first, then opens the pause sheet; a second BACK returns to the host. No confirmation is needed because no progress is lost.

**12. Feedback.** Matches the record ("Gentle positive visual confirmation on success. Neutral, non-punitive feedback on errors with option to try again. No timers or pressure."). Correct: one of 6 `cheers`, the scene fills (🌞), soft `correct` sound. Miss: neutral wobble plus `tryagain` taps; never red, never "wrong".

**13. Session time.** About 30-45 s per round; 5 rounds take about 3-4 min (the parent can set 4). Target 1.5-4 min (record estimate 1.5-3.5 min).

**14. Assets / audio / content.** Step cards with labels (screen text), page-turn sound. All spoken lines are pre-recorded clips (Rishi), with voice only after 🔊. Content: `catalogue/catalogue_games.json` → `ww092` (30 rounds, 3 levels).

**15. Accessibility.** Meaning is carried by the pictures, not by colour alone; labels are screen text for the parent only.  Plus: focus ring #F59E0B 8 px plus glow, targets at least 240 px, reduced-motion flag honoured, all audio optional, no reading required.

**16. Offline / failure.** Fully bundled; no network. If content fails to load or validate, the card is hidden and an error screen returns to the host.

**17. Parent options / data.** Enable/disable, rounds per set (4 or 5), reset. Stored locally: current level per game only.

**18. Tests.** Validator: `validate_games.py` (schema, answer keys, 8-word prompts, Unicode 11 emoji, TTS rules). Each routine has one defensible order (steps never swap safely); items distinct; labels match items; 3-5 items. Plus host contract tests: launch blocked when disabled or out of budget; budget expiry in instructions, play, feedback and pause leads to a calm end; BACK in every state; cold start in airplane mode; a real-remote pass on the OnePlus TV.

**19. Evidence and limits.** Background: routine sequencing supports independence and early planning (NCF-FS, NCERT 2022). Limitation: framework, not effect evidence. We claim only practice of the skill inside the activity.
