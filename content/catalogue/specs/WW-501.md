# WW-501 · Teen Rocket (our id `ww501`)
Status: **design_spec_draft** (awaiting father/reviewer approval) · Category: C. Mathematics & Numerical Thinking / Numbers 11-20 · Variant: `pick`

**1. Identity.** Original title "Teen Rocket", catalogue id WW-501. Batch b13 (worksheet-inspired play, 24 Nov 2026). Skill borrowed from worksheet sites; all text, pictures and layouts are our own.

**2. Age band.** Kindergarten (UKG); fits him.

**3. Objective (observable).** He reads a ten-frame (a full ten of gold stars plus extras) and picks the teen number; later he compares two teen numbers.

**4. What he practises.** Place value idea of ten and ones, counting on from ten, comparing numbers. (Source skill: "Numbers 11-20 as ten and some more (ten-frames)".)

**5. Originality and dedup.** Make Ten adds to ten; Number Explorer counts to 10. Teen numbers as ten plus ones are new. Decision: own game.

**6. Core loop.** Board: two rows of five gold stars (a ten-frame), a plus sign (L1 only), then the extra stars. He picks the number.
Example round: `ww501-1-01` (board 🌟x5, 🌟x5, ➕, ⭐, choices around 11). Spoken: "A full ten, and three more. How many stars in all?"

**7. Child-facing instruction.** Visual demo by {buddy} (Astro) in round 1 of the first session. Narration only on 🔊: "{buddy} is loading the rocket with stars! Count a full ten and some more." Prompt text 8 words or fewer (he can't read; it is for the parent).

**8. Remote mapping.** LEFT/RIGHT over the choices (or the object and its baskets), OK confirms, UP 🔊, BACK pause. Initial focus: 🔊 (pulsing) on the first round, then the first choice. Exactly one element is focused.

**9. Difficulty.** L1 ten plus 1-3. L2 ten plus 1-10 in mixed order (11-20). L3 which of two (or three) teen numbers is more. Levels: L1 Ten and a few 🌟; L2 Any teen number 🔢; L3 Which has more? 🆚.

**10. Hint / retry / recovery.** After 1 miss the choice fades and the `hint` is shown (voice only on 🔊). After 2 misses the answer is shown and he presses it ("You kept going!"). 🔊 always replays the line. Nothing is lost.

**11. Success, session end, early exit.** Session = 5 rounds, then the end_lines and the Play again / Back to games buttons. BACK cancels any local action, then opens the pause sheet; a second BACK returns to the host. No progress is lost.

**12. Feedback.** Correct: one of 6 `cheers`, soft `correct` sound. Miss: neutral wobble plus `tryagain` taps; never red, never "wrong". No timers, scores or streaks.

**13. Session time.** About 20-40 s per round; 5 rounds take about 2-3 min.

**14. Assets / audio / content.** Space theme: Astro the kid astronaut ({buddy}), the rocket, three planets and moon bases. Emoji (Unicode 11 or earlier, no ZWJ) in white candy cards. Spoken lines pre-recorded (Rishi), voice only after 🔊. Content: `catalogue/catalogue_games.json` → `ww501` (36 rounds, rounds per level [12, 12, 12]).

**15. Accessibility.** Meaning is carried by pictures, never by colour only or by reading. Focus ring #F59E0B 8 px plus glow, targets at least 240 px, reduced-motion honoured, all audio optional.

**16. Offline / failure.** Fully bundled; no network. If content fails to validate, the card is hidden and an error screen returns to the host.

**17. Parent options / data.** Enable/disable, rounds per set (4 or 5), reset. Stored locally: current level per game only.

**18. Tests.** `validate_games.py` (schema, answer keys, 8-word prompts, Unicode 11 emoji, TTS rules). Each round has one defensible answer; choices distinct; answer positions vary. Plus the host contract tests and a real-remote pass on the OnePlus TV.

**19. Evidence and limits.** Framework-level support only (NCF-FS, NCERT 2022 foundational goals). Limitation: framework, not effect evidence. We claim only practice of the skill inside the activity.
