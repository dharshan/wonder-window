# WW-011 · Number Train (our id `ww011`)
Status: **design_spec_draft** (awaiting father/reviewer approval) · Category: C. Mathematics & Numerical Thinking / Number Sequencing · Variant: `order`

**1. Identity.** Original title "Number Train", catalogue id WW-011, legacy alias C005. Source record unchanged in master.json.

**2. Age band.** Record primary 5-6 is not in age_groups (4-6, 5-7); resolved as 5-6. Fits him.

**3. Objective (observable).** He arranges 3-5 number carriages from smallest to largest (L3: non-consecutive numbers up to 10).

**4. What he practises.** Number order; comparing which is smaller; at L3, ordering with gaps (2, 5, 7, 10). (Source objective: "Place numbers in correct ascending order".)

**5. Originality and dedup.** Number Explorer finds one missing number (pick). Here he builds the whole order (order variant), a different action. Legacy C005 is kept as an alias. Decision: **own game**.

**6. Core loop.** Shuffled carriages below and the engine at the left of the track. Each OK sends the chosen carriage to the next track slot if it is the next smallest; otherwise it wobbles back. When full, the train chugs off.
Example rounds:
- L1 `ww011-1-01`: "Put the numbers in order!", order 1 2 3. Spoken: "Help the train! Put the carriages in order, from the smallest number to the biggest."
- L2 `ww011-2-01`: "Put the numbers in order!", order 1 2 3 4. Spoken: "Help the train! Put the carriages in order, from the smallest number to the biggest."
- L3 `ww011-3-01`: "Smallest to biggest!", order 1 3 5. Spoken: "Some numbers are missing this time. Put the carriages in order, smallest to biggest."

**7. Child-facing instruction.** Visual demo by the buddy (Pip) in round 1 of the first session. Narration only on 🔊: "The number train is at the station! Put the carriages in order so it can go. Choo choo!" Prompt text 8 words or fewer (he can't read; it is for the parent).

**8. Remote mapping.** LEFT/RIGHT over the remaining cards, OK = send; UP 🔊; BACK pause. Placed cards are not focusable. Initial focus: 🔊 (pulsing) on the first round, then the first choice. Focus never lands on an inert element; exactly one element is focused.

**9. Difficulty.** L1 1-9 in runs of 3 (dots under the numbers for rounds 1-6), L2 runs of 4-5, L3 non-consecutive. Levels: L1 One, two, three 🚃, L2 Up to five 🚋, L3 Missing numbers 🚄.

**10. Hint / retry / recovery.** After 2 wrong picks: the next correct carriage gently bounces once. 🔊 always replays the line. After the second miss the answer is shown and he presses it ("You kept going!"). Nothing is lost.

**11. Success, session end, early exit.** 5 trains per set. Each set is 5 rounds. BACK cancels any local action first (palette, peek), then opens the pause sheet; a second BACK returns to the host. No confirmation is needed because no progress is lost.

**12. Feedback.** Matches the record ("Train gently moves forward or celebrates on completion. Soft guidance if out of order."). Correct: one of 6 `cheers`, the scene fills (🚂), soft `correct` sound. Miss: neutral wobble plus `tryagain` taps; never red, never "wrong".

**13. Session time.** About 25-40 s per round; about 2.5-3.5 min. Target 1.5-4 min (record estimate 2-4 min).

**14. Assets / audio / content.** Train engine, carriages with Andika numerals, a 'chug' sound. All spoken lines are pre-recorded clips (Rishi), with voice only after 🔊. Content: `catalogue/catalogue_games.json` → `ww011`.

**15. Accessibility.** Numbers at 140 px or larger; dot labels for early rounds; no timing. Plus: focus ring #F59E0B 8 px plus glow, targets at least 240 px, reduced-motion flag honoured, all audio optional, no reading required.

**16. Offline / failure.** Fully bundled; no network. If content fails to load or validate, the card is hidden and an error screen returns to the host.

**17. Parent options / data.** Enable/disable, number range (to 5 or to 10), reset. Stored locally: current level per game only. Reset clears it without touching time settings.

**18. Tests.** Validator: `validate_games.py` (schema, answer keys, 8-word prompts, Unicode 11 emoji, TTS rules). Items are strictly increasing; the engine shuffle is deterministic and never shows the solved order; the wrong-pick wobble never moves focus away. Plus host contract tests: launch blocked when disabled or out of budget; budget expiry in instructions, play, feedback and pause leads to a calm end; BACK in every state; cold start in airplane mode; a real-remote pass on the OnePlus TV.

**19. Evidence and limits.** Clements & Sarama (2014), counting and number-ordering trajectories. Limitation: background only. We claim only practice of the skill inside the activity.
