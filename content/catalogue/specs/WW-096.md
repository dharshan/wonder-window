# WW-096 · Memory Grid (our id `ww096`)
Status: **design_spec_draft** (awaiting father/reviewer approval) · Category: B. Memory & Concentration / Visual Memory · Variant: `pairs`

**1. Identity.** Original title "Memory Grid", catalogue id WW-096. Source record unchanged in master.json.

**2. Age band.** Record 6-8; at 5½ L1-L2 (3-4 pairs) fit him and L3-L4 are a stretch; a grid of 6 pairs is the cap.

**3. Objective (observable).** He flips two cards at a time and finds all the matching pairs by remembering where each picture is.

**4. What he practises.** Visuospatial memory: holding where pictures are while attending to the next move. (Source objective: "Remember the positions of several objects on a small grid after they are hidden".)

**5. Originality and dedup.** Memory Tray (ww015) detects a changed item on one tray; Shape Echo / Sound Sequence repeat an order. Here he remembers positions on a grid and matches twins (the `pairs` engine variant). Different action. Decision: **own game**.

**6. Core loop.** Face-down grid; focus moves over the cards; OK flips; a match stays open with a soft chime; a miss turns both back after a calm beat. The round ends when all pairs are found.
Example rounds:
- L1 `ww096-1-01`: "Find the matching pairs!", cards 🍓 🍐 🍍 (3 pairs, 3 columns). Spoken: "Flip two cards. If they match, they stay open. Remember where things hide! Today: fruits."
- L2 `ww096-2-01`: "Find the matching pairs!", cards 🍌 🍒 🍉 🍇 (4 pairs, 4 columns). Spoken: "Flip two cards. If they match, they stay open. Remember where things hide! Today: fruits."
- L3 `ww096-3-01`: "Find the matching pairs!", cards 🍒 🍇 🥭 🍎 🍍 (5 pairs, 5 columns). Spoken: "Flip two cards. If they match, they stay open. Remember where things hide! Today: fruits."

**7. Child-facing instruction.** Visual demo by Cherry in round 1 of the first session. Narration only on 🔊: "Cherry turned her cards face down. Flip two cards and remember where each twin hides!" Prompt text 8 words or fewer (he can't read; it is for the parent).

**8. Remote mapping.** D-pad moves over the card grid (wraps by row), OK flips a card, UP 🔊, BACK pause. Two flipped cards that differ turn back after a calm beat (no timer pressure; he can press OK to flip them early). Initial focus: 🔊 (pulsing) on the first round, then the first choice. Focus never lands on an inert element; exactly one element is focused.

**9. Difficulty.** L1 3 pairs (6 cards, 3 columns), L2 4 pairs (8 cards), L3 5 pairs (10 cards, 5 columns), L4 6 pairs (12 cards, 4 columns). Ten themes (fruits, animals, vehicles, sea, food, sky, garden, sports, things we use, shapes). Levels: L1 Three pairs 🃏; L2 Four pairs 🎴; L3 Five pairs 🧩; L4 Six pairs 🧠.

**10. Hint / retry / recovery.** Nothing is ever lost on a mismatch. After 3 mismatches in a row one card of a pair glows softly (hint). After 2 more, one pair is turned over for him ("Look, a twin pair!"). 🔊 replays the line.

**11. Success, session end, early exit.** Session = 3 rounds, then the end_lines and the Play again / Back to games buttons. Early exit any time via BACK: it cancels any local action first, then opens the pause sheet; a second BACK returns to the host. No confirmation is needed because no progress is lost.

**12. Feedback.** Matches the record ("Soft positive confirmation. Errors receive neutral feedback and the chance to try again. No negative sounds or messages."). Correct: one of 6 `cheers`, the scene fills (🧠), soft `correct` sound. Miss: neutral wobble plus `tryagain` taps; never red, never "wrong".

**13. Session time.** About 40-70 s per round; a set is 3 rounds (about 2-4 min). Target 1.5-4 min (record estimate 1.5-4 min).

**14. Assets / audio / content.** Card backs (Sky Garden leaf pattern), 10 themed emoji pools, flip and chime sound. Engine duplicates and shuffles `cards`. All spoken lines are pre-recorded clips (Rishi), with voice only after 🔊. Content: `catalogue/catalogue_games.json` → `ww096` (40 rounds, 4 levels).

**15. Accessibility.** Meaning is carried by the pictures, not by colour alone; labels are screen text for the parent only. Every theme uses visually distinct pictures; no two pictures of a pool look alike. A sound plays on each flip so it is not vision-only. Plus: focus ring #F59E0B 8 px plus glow, targets at least 240 px, reduced-motion flag honoured, all audio optional, no reading required.

**16. Offline / failure.** Fully bundled; no network. If content fails to load or validate, the card is hidden and an error screen returns to the host.

**17. Parent options / data.** Enable/disable, starting level, grid size cap, reset. Stored locally: current level per game only.

**18. Tests.** Validator: `validate_games.py` (schema, answer keys, 8-word prompts, Unicode 11 emoji, TTS rules). `cards` are 3-8 distinct emoji (engine duplicates and shuffles); `cols` 3-5; every theme uses only Unicode 11 emoji; the pair cannot be flipped twice by mistake (a face-up card is inert). Plus host contract tests: launch blocked when disabled or out of budget; budget expiry in instructions, play, feedback and pause leads to a calm end; BACK in every state; cold start in airplane mode; a real-remote pass on the OnePlus TV.

**19. Evidence and limits.** Gathercole, S. E. & Alloway, T. P. (2008), Working Memory and Learning (SAGE), background on short-term visual memory. Limitation: meta-analyses (Melby-Lervåg & Hulme, 2013, Developmental Psychology) find memory-training does not transfer broadly, so we claim only practice of this task. We claim only practice of the skill inside the activity.
