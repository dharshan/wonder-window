# WW-238 · Build Time
Status: **deferred** · Category: E. Creativity & Imagination / Design Challenges · Source age 4-6 · Variant: `compose (new)`

**Objective (source).** Design something within given soft constraints

**Decision.** **deferred: needs variant `compose`**.

**Objective (observable).** He builds a small thing on a grid (a house, a garden, a boat) that must follow one or two soft rules ('it needs a door', 'use only two colours'), choosing the parts himself.

**What he practises.** Planning within a rule, choosing parts on purpose, making something of his own. (Source objective: "Design something within given soft constraints".)

**Originality and dedup.** Best Choice (ww080) picks one answer; Invent Adventure (ww150) has no rule and no building; Symmetry Butterfly (ww019) fills a mirrored grid. None places parts under a constraint. Cluster WW-DUP-061 (WW-222 Art Challenge, WW-283 Dream Maker) follows this spec. Decision: **deferred: needs variant `compose`**.

**Schema proposal.** Variant `compose` as in specs/WW-133.md, mode `build`, with `target: null` (free build) plus a new field `must_include` (1-2 parts that must appear at least once, e.g. ["🚪"]) and `min_parts`; 'Done' enables only when both hold. The hint highlights the missing required part; never wrong, never timed. Validator: every `must_include` part is in `parts`; `min_parts` 2-6; 3-8 rounds per level are all free builds, so L1 uses a one-part rule.

**Age, remote, hints.** Source age 4-6. LEFT/RIGHT/UP/DOWN to move, OK to choose or place, BACK undo then pause; no timers, no wrong answers; fully bundled and offline. No game content was written in batch b9.
