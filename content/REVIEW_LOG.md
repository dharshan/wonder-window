# Content needing human review

Safety-related rounds are written by an agent but must be checked by the project owner (a parent) before a public release. A round stays `pending` until the owner records a decision here; `release` is blocked while any row is `pending` (to be enforced by the release checklist, not yet automated).

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| `me-3-21` | A stranger offers sweets: say no, go to your grown-up | Stranger-safety advice; must not frighten | pending (owner) |
| `me-2-11` | Reading a simple picture map (no real places) | Replaces a home-address round; must not invite real locations | pending (owner) |
| `me-3-22` | Finding the way on a picture map (no real places) | Replaces a home-location round; must not invite real locations | pending (owner) |

How to record a decision: change the status to `approved (owner, YYYY-MM-DD)` or `changes requested` with a one-line note, in a PR that touches only this file and the round.
