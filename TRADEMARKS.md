# Name, logo and mascot policy

"Wonder Window", its logo and its default mascot "Pip" identify this project and its child-safety promises: no ads, no tracking, no streaks or earned screen time, parent-approved content and one shared screen-time limit. They are not registered trademarks; this file is a usage policy for the community.

- You may use the name to refer to the project and to distribute unmodified builds.
- A modified fork must not imply it is the official project, and must not use the name, logo or mascot in a way that could mislead parents.
- A fork that changes any child-safety, privacy or screen-time rule (AGENTS.md, docs/CHILD_SAFETY.md, docs/SESSION_TIME_POLICY.md) must use a different name and mascot.
- Families can rename the app and buddy for their own TV in `family.properties` (family edition); that is a personal setting, not a fork.
