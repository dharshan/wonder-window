# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free community. Be constructive, patient and collaborative. Harassment, discrimination, sexualised content directed at minors, doxxing, threats, intimidation, personal attacks and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

This applies in project spaces and when representing the project. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation proportionately. Report concerns privately to maintainers. Add a monitored contact before public launch.

Adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
